ORAL ORDER
2 02-01-2023 1.  The  petitioner  prays  for  compassionate
appointment on the ground of death of his father who expired
while in service. It is stated that the petitioner had applied for
the post as his father expired on 22.03.2005 in an accident while
working as clerk at Primary Health Centre Belaganj. His
application form was forwarded. However, an objection has
been raised by the Respondents on the ground that his mother is
in service.
2. The petitioner has contended that his mother is not
residing with the petitioner because his mother was separated
from his father much earlier. However, no document in this
regard of degree of separation or divorce has been placed before


Patna High Court CWJC No.23811 of 2012(2) dt.02-01-2023
2/2 
this Court. The mere statement of the petitioner that his mother
was separately residing from his father, would not be a
sufficient ground to ignore the serving parent of the petitioner.
3.  The  writ  petition  is  mis-concieved  and  is
accordingly dismissed.
