Petitioner has prayed for the following relief(s):
“i) For quashing the order vide letter
No. 1330 dated 31.08.2018 (Annexure-5) by
which the Respondent Executive Engineer has
terminated  the  Agreement  (Agreement  No.
97/SBD 2009-10) and forfeited the Security
Deposits arbitrarily without issuance of any
show cause/explanation to the petitioner.
ii)  For  issuance  of  the  Writ  of
Mandamus  commanding  the  Respondent
Executive  Engineer  to  return  the  forfeited


Patna High Court CWJC No.17615 of 2019 dt.02-01-2023
2/3 
Security Deposits to the petitioner.
iii) For direction to the Respondent
Executive Engineer to make payments to the
petitioner for the two years maintenance work
(2014-2015, and 2015-2016).
                And/or
Pass  such  order/orders  as  Your
Lordships may deem fit and proper under the
facts and circumstances of the case.”
Vide  impugned  orders  dated  24.08.2018
(Annexure-5, page-83) and 20th of September, 2019 (Annexure-
6, page-93), petitioner stands debarred from participating in
bidding process, without any period of debarment being fixed.
The contract entered between the petitioner and the
respondent stands rescinded and steps initiated for debarring the
petitioner.
Ordinarily, this Court would not have entertained
the petition, leaving it for the parties to take recourse to the
remedies in terms of the agreement, but in view of the peculiar
facts and circumstances, when the impugned orders entail civil
consequences and is in utter violation of principles of natural
justice, more so when a similar order in relation to 59
contractors was passed without touching the case of each one of
the petitioners therein, we quash and set aside the impugned


Patna High Court CWJC No.17615 of 2019 dt.02-01-2023
3/3 
orders dated 24.08.2018 and 20.09.2019 (Annexures-5 and 6)
passed by respondent No. 4, namely Executive Engineer, Rural
Works Department, Works Division, Boring Road, Patna with
the direction to the petitioner to make himself available in the
office of the Executive Engineer, Rural Works Department,
Works Division, Boring Road, Patna on 16.01.2023 at 11:00
a.m. on which date an opportunity shall be afforded to the
petitioner to place on record any material which the petitioner
chooses to do so in his defence and the respondent shall pass a
fresh order in view of the attending facts and circumstances.
Liberty reserved to the petitioner to agitate the
issue, should the need so arise subsequently.
Petition is disposed of in the aforesaid terms. 
Interlocutory Application(s), if any, shall stand
disposed of.
