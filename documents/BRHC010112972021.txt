ORAL ORDER
(Per: HONOURABLE MR. JUSTICE ASHUTOSH KUMAR)
3 03-01-2023   Heard Mr. Binay Kumar, the learned Advocate
for the petitioner and Mr. Upendra Pratap Singh, the
learned counsel for the State. 
The license of the petitioner was suspended vide
order dated 20.04.2020 and an explanation was asked for
from the petitioner. Barely, seven days after, the license of
the petitioner has been cancelled on the plea that the
notice to the petitioner was not replied by him. 
The learned counsel for the petitioner has made a


Patna High Court CWJC No.5703 of 2021(3) dt.03-01-2023
2/3 
specific statement in the writ petition that no notice was
ever served upon him. 
This contention gets buttressed by the fact that
within seven days, the order of cancellation of his license
has been passed. 
We are of the view that the petitioner must get
an opportunity of explaining his cause before his license is
cancelled. 
For the afore-noted reason, we set aside the
order of cancellation of license dated 27.04.2020. The
order of suspension of license shall, but remain inviolate. 
The matter is remitted to the Licensing Authority,
who  shall issue fresh notice to the petitioner within a
period of 15 days form the date of receipt / production of
a copy of this order, giving reasonable time to the
petitioner to represent his cause. 
The petitioner shall reply to the said show cause
notice within 30 days of the receipt of the notice. 
Thereafter, the Licensing Authority shall, after
adverting to the reply of the petitioner, pass a reasoned


Patna High Court CWJC No.5703 of 2021(3) dt.03-01-2023
3/3 
order within further period of 60 days. 
With the afore-noted direction / observation, the
writ petition stands disposed of.
