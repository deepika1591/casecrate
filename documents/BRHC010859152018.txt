2 02-01-2023 Heard the learned counsel for the parties.
Mr. Sanat Kumar Mishra, the learned Advocate for the
petitioner  submits  that  notwithstanding  the  fact  that  the
petitioner  was  adjudged  the  most  suitable  person  for  being
granted license, the license was granted to respondent No.9 and
not to him on the sole plea that one of his brothers, who is not
part  of  the  joint  family,  was  a  licensee  from  before  and,
therefore, the disqualification under Clause-11 would not apply

Patna High Court CWJC No.12504 of 2018(2) dt.02-01-2023
2/2 
to him. 
In view of the nature of controversy  raised and in
view  of  the  notification  dated  21.07.2022  issued  by  the
Government in exercise of the powers conferred under Sections
3  and  5  of  the  Essential  Commodities  Act,  1955  read  with
Clause-36  of  the  Bihar  Targeted  Public  Distribution  System
(Control)  Order,  2016,  we  direct  that  in  the  event  of  the
petitioner making a suitable representation/complaint before the
concerned Divisional Commissioner within a period of 30 days,
the Divisional Commissioner shall look into the matter and after
hearing all the stakeholders, including respondent No.9, shall
pass an appropriate order within next 60 days. 
We have specifically asked for respondent No.9 to be
heard by the Divisional Commissioner for the reason that this
order has been passed in his absence.
With the afore-noted direction, the writ petition stands
disposed off.
