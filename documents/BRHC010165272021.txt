CORAM: HONOURABLE MR. JUSTICE ANIL KUMAR SINHA
ORAL ORDER
2 02-01-2023   Submission of learned counsel for the petitioner is
that adequate compensation has not been given to the petitioner
for acquisition of his land for the construction of National High
Way.  The  petitioner  has  filed  a  claim  for  enhanced
compensation  before  the  Arbitrator  i.e.,  Divisional
Commissioner-cum-Arbitrator, Purnea under Section 3G5 of
National High Way Act, 1956 bearing Arbitration Case No.
211/2019.
The submission is that for the last three years no
development has taken place in the Arbitration Case filed by the


Patna High Court CWJC No.7104 of 2021(2) dt.02-01-2023
2/2 
petitioner and a direction be issued to the Arbitrator to decide
the claim of the petitioner within a reasonable time. 
Taking into consideration the nature of prayer made
by the petitioner and the fact that the Arbitration Case is
pending since 2019, the learned Divisional Commissioner-cum-
Arbitrator, Purnea is directed to dispose the Arbitration Case
No. 211/2019 within a period of six months in accordance with
law. 
With the aforesaid direction and observation, the
instant petition stands disposed of.
