ORAL ORDER
2 02-01-2023 1. The petitioner assails the order passed by the
Respondents on 08.03.1999 and 03.04.1999 whereby the
petitioners were disallowed to continue on the post on the
ground of their appointment being found to be not in accordance
with law. The writ petition has been preferred by the petitioners
in the year 2012 which is being taken up now in 2023. Almost
twenty four years has passed away. The laches on the part of the
petitioner and the delay has resulted in the writ petition having


Patna High Court CWJC No.23585 of 2012(2) dt.02-01-2023
2/2 
become otiose and no relief can be granted to the petitioner of
reinstatement after a long period of twenty four years. No
reasons have come forward for coming to the Court after such a
long period.
2. The writ petition is accordingly dismissed.
