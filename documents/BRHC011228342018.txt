CORAM: HONOURABLE MR. JUSTICE ANIL KUMAR SINHA
ORAL ORDER
3 03-01-2023   It is an admitted position between the parties that a
compromise decree was passed in Partition Suit No. 47/1952
and the Respondent No. 5 being aggrieved by the same has filed
a Miscellaneous Case under Order 23A Rule 1 CPC bearing
Miscellaneous Case No. 06/2016. 
The contention between the parties is with regard to
mutation of the subject land and as per the order passed by


Patna High Court CWJC No.24245 of 2018(3) dt.03-01-2023
3/3 
revisional authority mutation stands in the name of the
Respondent No. 5. 
Be that as it may, this writ application is disposed
with a direction to Sub Judge 1st/ Successor Court, Begusarai to
decide the Miscellaneous Case No. 06/2016 in accordance with
law and on its own merit within a reasonable time frame. 
It is made clear that the mutation order, if any, shall be
subject to the final outcome of the Miscellaneous Case decided
by the Civil Court.
