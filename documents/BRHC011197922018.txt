The  petitioner  no.1  is  the  Fair  Price  Dealer’s
Association whereas the petitioners nos. 2 to 7 are the individual
licencees under the public distribution system. Their only
grievance is that the food-grains for the month of February,
2017 was not allotted to them even though they had made
payments for the same in the month of March, 2017.
Surprisingly, the petitioners remained the licensees
under the system and the money which was deposited by them
in March, 2017 for lifting of food-grains for the month of
February, 2017 was neither returned to them nor was it adjusted
against supply for other succeeding months.
The learned counsel for the petitioners has also
pointed out that on behalf of the association of the dealers, the
matter was brought to the notice of the authorities but till date,
no decision has been taken. The writ petition was filed within
time in the year 2018 when the entreaties of the petitioners for
either return of such amount or its adjustment for the next
succeeding months fell on deaf ears. 
Under the circumstances, we deem it appropriate to
direct that in case the individual petitioners/licencees approach
the District Magistrate with a suitable representation within a
period of 30 days, the District Magistrate shall have the facts

inquired and ascertained and in case, it is found that the claim of
the petitioners is tenable, he shall make necessary orders or
cause necessary sequel orders to be passed either for returning
the amount to the respective licencees or adjusting such amount
for other months as the petitioners, according to their averments
made in the writ petition, are still licencees under the system. 
The decision shall be taken by the Collector within 60
days of the filing of suitable petition as referred to above and
bringing to his/her notice a copy of this order.
With the afore-noted direction/observation, the writ
petitioner stands disposed off.
