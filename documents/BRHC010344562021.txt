Pleadings has already been completed in this case.
Vide order dated 15.11.2022, learned counsel for the
petitioner seeks three weeks time to file I.A.
Vide order dated 06.12.2022 two weeks further time
was granted.
From the records it transpires that no I.A. is present
on the record.
Present application is being filed for grant of payment
of Death-cum-Retiral Benefit, Family Pension, Gratuity, G.P.F.,
Group  Insurance  and  other  benefits  as  the  petitioners  are
entitled.
Petitioner  no.1  claims  herself  to  be  the  wife  of
deceased employee and petitioner no.2 claims himself to be the
son of said employee. The deceased employee was appointed on
compassionate ground as Peon in the year 1992, joined services
on 12.01.1993 and expired on 24.11.2020. Petitioner no.1 and 2
filed representations dated 13.03.2021 through speed post.
Learned counsel for  the State submits that in the
official records the name of petitioner no.1 is not figured as well
as petitioner no.1 is not the mother of petitioner no.2. In this
regard several letters were issued from the office of Executive

Patna High Court CWJC No.10885 of 2021(4) dt.02-01-2023
3/3 
Engineer  (Water  Resources  Department),  Punpun  Flood
Protection Division,  Patna City, Patna so that the  claim of
deceased  employee  in  favour  of  his  heirs  and  legal
representatives may be decided in accordance with law.
A letter dated 17.03.2021 has been issued to petitioner
no.1 which is annexed as Annexure-C in the counter affidavit
but she did not give any response thereafter.
In the above stated background, I am disposing of the
present application with a direction to the petitioners to file their
representation  before  respondent  no.6  who  shall  decide  the
claim  of  petitioners  in  accordance  with  law  and  without
prejudice from passing of order by the present Court within 60
days.
With this direction the writ application is disposed off.
