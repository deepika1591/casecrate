CORAM: HONOURABLE MR. JUSTICE DR. ANSHUMAN
ORAL ORDER
4 02-01-2023     Heard learned counsel for the petitioner and learned
counsel for the respondent Zila Parishad, Bhojpur at Ara and
learned APP for the State.
The present application has been filed for making
payment of retiral dues amounting Rs. 98,203/- to the petitioner
being the grand son of Late Dev Kumar Ram. During pendency
of the writ petition, a letter vide Memo No. 791 dated
08.12.2022 has been served upon the counsel for the petitioner
during winter vacation by which the claim of the petitioner has
been rejected with a direction to obtain a succession certificate

Patna High Court CWJC No.7750 of 2021(4) dt.02-01-2023
2/2 
from the competent court and then to claim reliefs according to
rule. In this view of the matter, counsel for the petitioner
submits that he seeks permission to withdraw the writ petition
with liberty to move in accordance with law.
Learned counsel for Zila Parishad Bhojpur at Ara has
annexed the office order dated 08.12.2022 which is on record as
Annexure-B.
The present writ application has been permitted to
withdraw and a direction has been given to the Zila Parishad
that after obtaining the succession certificate, if the petitioner
files the said claim then the Zila Parishad shall make payment
within 3 months thereafter.
With this direction, this writ application is disposed
off.
