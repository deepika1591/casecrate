ORAL ORDER
2 02-01-2023   Learned  counsel  for  the  petitioner  submits  that
Chakbandi was conducted in Dinara Block, Rohtas in which it
has been confirmed that the land in question is in the name of
affiliated  college,  namely,  Ram  Narain  Sah  Sarvodya
Mahavidyalaya, Dinara, District, Rohtas.
According  to  the  petitioner,  after  Chakbandi
Chakkhata  was  published  on  21.03.1994  and  name  of  the
college is mentioned with regard to 3.50 Acre in Chakkhata 623
and 1.96 Acre in Chakkhata No. 651 of Bhadsara Mauza and
2.03 Acre in Gaurasara Mauza. 
The contention is that in Register-II the College land

situated in Bhadsara Mauza has been described as “Anabad
Bihar Sarkar” and the college land situated in Gaurasara Mauza
has been described as “Anabad Sarvasadharan”.
The petitioner made contact with the Circle Officer,
Dinara to take necessary steps for correction in Register-II but
nothing has been done. 
Taking into consideration the nature of contention,
this writ petition is disposed of with a direction to the petitioner
to  file  an  appropriate  petition/application  before  the  Circle
Officer,  Dinara  Block,  District-Rohtas  for  correction  of
Register-II within a period of one month. 
If such an application is filed, the C.O, Dinara Block,
District-Rohtas, is obliged to dispose the same in accordance
with  law  after  taking  into  consideration  all  the  materials
available to him.
