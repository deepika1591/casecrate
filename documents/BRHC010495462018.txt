ORAL ORDER
7 02-01-2023 1. The petitioner has preferred this writ petition
alleging that he was selected for the post of Shiksha Swam Sevi/
Tola Sevak by the Selection Committee on 01.08.2015, but was
not allowed to join on the post.
2. Learned counsel for the petitioner submits that the
action of the respondents were arbitrary and unjustified. There


Patna High Court CWJC No.3336 of 2018(7) dt.02-01-2023
2/3 
was no reason to stop the petitioner from joining as Tola Sevak
after he was already issued appointment order. 
  3. P er contra, learned counsel appearing for the State
submits,  the petitioner was asked to join the training which he
has failed to join and, therefore, his joining in the school  was
not accepted. 
              4. I have considered the submissions, although, the post
is under scheme and ordinarily this Court would not entertain
writ  petitions  relating  to  such  contractual  appointments.
However, in the present case it is noticed that the petitioner was
issued appointment letter where after he was not allowed to join.
The plea taken by the respondent of petitioner having not joined
the training is found to be without basis. A person sent for
training after he is allowed join.  Accordingly, the respondents
were required to follow the order of appointment issued by the
Committee dated 01.08.2015 and thereafter, sent the person for
training. As those who are sent for training are those who are
already on the rolls and not outsiders.
          5. In view of above, this writ petition is allowed. The
verbal order of the respondents not allowing the petitioner to
join is set aside. Petitioner shall be free to submit his joining
within 7 days from today, thereafter,  the State shall sent the


Patna High Court CWJC No.3336 of 2018(7) dt.02-01-2023
3/3 
petitioner for training as per their guidelines which were  in
existence  at that time in 2015/16. 
           6. The petitioner’s joining shall be treated from the day
appointment orders were issued dated 01.08.2015 notionally
however, actual salaries and benefits shall not be admissible to
the petitioner and his salary shall be released only  from the date
of actual joining.
           7. Writ petition is accordingly allowed.
