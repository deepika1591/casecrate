ORAL ORDER
10 04-01-2023 From  the  submission  advanced  on  behalf  of  the
petitioner, it appears that this Court's order dated 04.08.2014
passed in C.W.J.C. No. 23710 of 2012 has been complied with,
inasmuch as,  the petitioner has been reinstated in the service
and has been paid admissible amount of arrears of salary.
Learned  counsel  for  the  petitioner  has  however,
submitted that the calculation of arrears of salary done by the
respondents  is  inaccurate  and  not  based  on  the  actual
entitlements of the petitioner as a trained teacher. 
In a contempt proceeding I am not inclined to enter
into such dispute. It goes without saying that the petitioner shall
be  at  liberty  to  raise  his  grievance  before  the  appropriate
authority by making a representation claiming his entitlements if

any.
This contempt proceeding stands dropped.
