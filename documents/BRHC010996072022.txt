ORAL ORDER
2 03-01-2023 Heard the parties.
The petitioner has come before this Court seeking
correction of his date of birth in his certificate on the ground tht
the examination was conducted in 2012 while the date of birth
mentions year of birth as 2019.  The correction is required to be
made for which he has moved an application.
However, the counsel appearing for the Board wants
time to make proper corrections.
Keeping in view the law laid down in Jigya Yadav
Vrs. the CBSE & Ors., reported in (2021) 7 SCC 535 this
Court allows the Central Board of Secondary Education to make
suitable correction as per the record and issue a fresh certificate


Patna High Court CWJC No.17682 of 2022(2) dt.03-01-2023
2/2 
expeditiously, preferably within a period of two months.
The writ petition stands allowed.
