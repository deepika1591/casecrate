CORAM: HONOURABLE MR. JUSTICE DR. ANSHUMAN
ORAL ORDER
4 03-01-2023 Heard Mr. Awadhesh Kumar, learned counsel for the
petitioner  and  Mr.  Ashhar  Mustafa  for  the  Tilka  Manjhi
Bhagalpur University, Bhagalpur.  
The present writ application has been filed for the
following reliefs :-
(i) For directing the respondent authorities to make
the entire payment of death-cum-retiral benefits of
the petitioner’s mother – Late Prabha Paul, who
was posted as Lecturer, Chemistry Department,
B.R.M.  College,  Madhopur,  Munger,  to  the
petitioner, who is the only living legal heir of her

Patna High Court CWJC No.9404 of 2021(4) dt.03-01-2023
2/5 
mother Late Prabha Paul who died in harness on
09.09.2017.
(ii) For directing the respondent authorities to
make the entire payments forthwith within a
reasonable time to the petitioner as she is in deep
financial lurch after the death of her mother and is
not being able to manage the financial expenses of
her education and is being taken care off by her
aunt (Mausi).
(iii) For grant of any other relief/reliefs as may
deem fit and proper in the light of the facts and
circumstances of the case.
Learned counsel for the petitioner submits that mother
of petitioner was posted as Reader in Balmiki Rajniti Mahila
College which is constituent unit of Tilka Manjhi Bhagalpur
University. She died due to cardiac arrest on 09.09.2017. He
submits that mother of petitioner was never married with any
one but the birth of petitioner was taken place due to love and
affectionate relationship between one Banshidhar Prasad with
her mother. It has been submitted that with the span of time said
Banshidhar Prasad started living separately and presently there
is no trace of Banshidhar Prasad. He also submits that mother of

Patna High Court CWJC No.9404 of 2021(4) dt.03-01-2023
3/5 
petitioner  was  promoted  to  the  post  of  Reader  w.e.f.  from
10.02.1992 and died due to cardiac arrest on 09.09.2017. This
fact was known to all, that there is no marriage between the
mother of petitioner with said Banshidhar Prasad. In the service
book, the mother  of  petitioner  has  mentioned  the  name  of
petitioner as nominee in column 7 for G.P.F. and column 9 for
gratuity  &  family  pension.  She  has  disclosed  the  name  of
petitioner as her nominee in bank account. These two entries
made in Annexure-5 & 6 are categorical indication and intention
of the employee (petitioner’s mother) that she was interested
that  her  entire  benefit  relating  to  services  be  given  to  her
daughter (the present petitioner).
Learned  counsel  for  petitioner  also  submits  that
petitioner has not made any suppression and submitted all those
documents before this Court, she has submitted her Aadhar
Card, Voter I.D. card, pan card, school certificates and birth
certificate.
Learned  counsel  for  the  petitioner  submits  that
petitioner is a student and after death of her mother, filed a
representation  to  provide  retiral  dues  and  family  pension
intimating  that she  is  the  only  living  successor/heir  of  her
mother and, therefore, demanded the entire retiral benefits of her

Patna High Court CWJC No.9404 of 2021(4) dt.03-01-2023
4/5 
mother in her favour.
Learned counsel for the University has filed counter
affidavit and submits that it is true that petitioner is the only
daughter and legal heir of her deceased mother but in the death
certificate of her mother, her father name was mentioned as
Banshidhar Prasad and only due to this reason, University is not
inclined to make payment to the petitioner.
After going through the pleadings of the parties, this
Court is of the view that admittedly the mother of petitioner was
Lecturer and subsequently become Reader in the College and
she consciously submitted the name of petitioner as nominee in
her G.P.F., gratuity, family pension as well as in her saving
account, these entries were made by the mother of petitioner
herself in her own handwriting which indicates her intention.
In this view of the matter, I am of the view that
petitioner is entitled to get the entire retiral dues in her favour.
The  University  namely  Tilka  Manjhi  Bhagalpur
University,  Bhagalpur  is  directed  to  make  payment  to  the
petitioner towards the relief sought in the writ petition within 30
days from the date of passing of this order.
Petitioner is also directed to file an indemnity before
the  University  for  payments  other  than  gratuity,  G.P.F.  &

With  this  direction,  the  writ  application  stands
