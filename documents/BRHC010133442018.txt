2 02-01-2023 Heard the learned counsel for the parties. 
The licence of the petitioner has been cancelled,
which order has been affirmed by the Appellate Authority.
After some arguments, Mr. Manoj Kumar Pandey, the
learned Advocate for the petitioner seeks permission to
withdraw this application in order to prefer a revision petition
against the orders passed by the licensing authority as also the
appellate authority cancelling the licence of the petitioner. 
Should such a revision petition be filed within a

With the afore-noted direction/observation, the writ
petition stands withdrawn and disposed off.
