CORAM: HONOURABLE MR. JUSTICE PURNENDU SINGH
ORAL ORDER
2 02-01-2023   Heard Mr. Mayank Mohan, learned counsel for the
petitioner and Mr. Vijay Kumar Singh No. 1, learned AC to SC-
18.
 The grievance of the petitioner in the present writ
petition is that the petitioner who is the wife of the deceased has
been denied compensation amount on account of death of her
husband due to Covid-19.
 Learned counsel appearing on behalf of the petitioner
submitted that the petitioner has submitted all the reports i.e.
medical certificate, admission and discharge certificates by the
government hospital and pathological test which confirms that
her husband died due to Covid-19. However, the application of


Patna High Court CWJC No. 7653 of 2022(2) dt.02-01-2023
2/3 
the petitioner has not been entertained by the Civil Surgeon,
Gopalganj-respondent no. 4. Learned counsel further submitted
that though blood sample for RTPCR test (Antigen test) was
taken  from  husband  of  petitioner  but  no  report  was
communicated on the registered mobile phone of her husband
who was suffering from Covid-19.
Learned counsel appearing on behalf of the State
submitted that petitioner can only claim compensation, if the
report confirms that the death was due to Covid-19 in terms of
government circular then only the petitioner is entitled for
compensation.
Considering the rival submissions of the parties, the
Civil Surgeon, District Health Society, Goplaganj is directed to
hold an enquiry at his level and verify all the documents
including Antigen report of the deceased, if it is found that any
one of the document confirms the death due to Covid-19 then in
that  case,  in  absence  of  Antigen  report,  the  compensation
amount  Rs.  4  lakhs  cannot  be  denied  in  view  of  the
announcement made by the State Government and the ex-gratia
amount of Rs. 50,000/-, announced by the Central Government
within a period of three weeks.
With above observation and direction, the present writ


Patna High Court CWJC No. 7653 of 2022(2) dt.02-01-2023
3/3 
petition is disposed of.
