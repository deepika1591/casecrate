CORAM: HONOURABLE MR. JUSTICE P. B. BAJANTHRI
ORAL ORDER
3 04-01-2023 CWJC No. 8015 of 2022
CWJC No. 8015 of 2022 is not maintainable in the
light of Section 8 – Recovery of Gratuity under the Payment of
Gratuity Act, 1972. In other words, petitioner has a statutory
remedy before the Controlling Authority for the purpose of
recovery of gratuity amount from the employer. In that regard,
Controlling Authority was required to issue certificate. On
receipt of certificate, the Jurisdictional Collector is required to
take further action insofar as recovery of gratuity from the
employer. 
Accordingly,  the  present  writ  petition  is  not
maintainable. Hence, writ petition CWJC No. 8015 of 2022
stands dismissed reserving liberty to the petitioner to approach
concerned authority.
