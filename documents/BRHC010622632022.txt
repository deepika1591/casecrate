3 02-01-2023   Petitioner has prayed for the following relief(s):-
“i. For issuance of writ in the nature of Man -
damus directing the respondents to comply
with order dated 21.08.2021 passed in Case
No. 401110125061903863 (Annexure-2) under
Bihar Public Grievances redressal Right Act by
the Sub-Divisional Public Grievances redressal


Patna High Court CWJC No.11775 of 2022(3) dt.02-01-2023
2/3 
Officer, Bettiah, West Champaran on the
ground that the same has been passed on merit
after hearing the both parties.
ii. For issuance of writ in the nature of Man -
damus directing the respondents to consider
the case of the petitioner and issue no due cer -
tificate to the petitioner after accepting his due
in view of order dated 21.8.2021 and also to
restore his electric connection.
iii. For a direction or issuance of appropriate
writ in the nature of Quo Warrant requiring the
respondent authorities to show as to what has
been done by them in the light of the order
dated 21.08.2021 passed under Bihar Public
Grievances redressal Right Act by the Sub-Di -
visional Public Grievances Office, Bettiah,
West Champaran and why petitioner's griev -
ance has not been dealt within the reasonable
time and his dues cleared by accepting his pay-
ment and issuing no dues certificate to the peti-
tioner.
iv. For a direction to respondents to make nec -
essary arrangement to correct the situation re -
garding the bill and the electricity connection
to the petitioner's mill and to restore the elec -
tricity.
v. And for any other reliefs for which to peti -
tioners be found entitled in the eye of law.”
Learned counsel for the petitioner seeks permission to
withdraw the present petition. 


Patna High Court CWJC No.11775 of 2022(3) dt.02-01-2023
3/3 
Permission granted. 
The instant petition stands disposed of as withdrawn.
