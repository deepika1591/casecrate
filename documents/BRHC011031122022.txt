ORAL ORDER
2 03-01-2023 Heard the parties.
 Learned Senior Counsel appearing for the petitioner
has submitted that after filing of the writ petition on 26.12.2022
the respondents have already conducted an inspection for
granting affiliation of post B.Sc. Nursing Course.  However, he
apprehends that no decision will be taken as was not taken
earlier.  It has been pointed out that also too in 2019 inspection
was conducted but no decision was taken at the level of the
respondents.
Learned Senior Counsel appearing for the State does
not object if this case is decided with observation that the


Patna High Court CWJC No.17698 of 2022(2) dt.03-01-2023
2/2 
decision be taken.
Having heard both the counsel, this Court is of the
firm view that a decision must be always taken if an application
is moved for seeking affiliation and the case of any institution
should not be kept in abeyance for years together.  
In  the  present  case,  this  Court  finds  that  the
application was filed on 01.10.2018 but no decision has been
taken till date.  However, as the new inspection has already been
conducted on 26.12.2022, this Court directs the respondents to
take steps on the basis of the inspection conducted.  If there are
deficiencies found the institution be asked to remove the
deficiency within a stipulated period and thereafter if it so
chooses conduct a fresh inspection and pass suitable order as
required in law.  The entire exercise should be conducted
positively within a period of three months henceforth.
With the aforesaid observations, this writ petition
stands disposed of.
