CORAM: HONOURABLE MR. JUSTICE DR. ANSHUMAN
ORAL ORDER
3 03-01-2023 Heard learned counsel for the petitioner and learned
counsel for the State. 
Learned counsel for the petitioner has filed the present
writ petition for directing the respondent authority to make
payment of retiral benefits i.e. 100 % Pension, Gratuity, Leave
encashment. Learned counsel for the petitioner submits that
petitioner was appointed as Music Teacher on 07.03.1983 and
she retired on 30.04.2018 after completion of 35 years of

service. Counsel submits that petitioner alongwith other teachers
terminated but they all were reinstated in compliance of the
order passed in C.W.J.C. 17495 of 2016 vide Memo No. 289
dated 17.02.2017. Counsel further submits that a departmental
proceeding was initiated against 7 teachers including one Kanta
Devi who retired on 31.07.2018. Counsel submits that the said
Kanta Devi is similarly situated to the petitioner to whom all the
retiral benefits were given but the benefit to the petitioner is
denied. 
Learned counsel for the petitioner submits that he has
filed representation vide Annexure-3 of the writ petition. A
counter affidavit has been filed before the Deputy Director of
Education, Kosi Division, Saharsa (respondent no. 6) but there
is no clear cut stand taken by him as to why the petitioner was
deprived from a retiral dues. 
In the above facts and circumstances, counsel for the
petitioner is directed to file a fresh representation before the
Director, Secondary Education, Bihar, Patna (respondent no. 3),
who upon going through the records of the petitioner as well as
similarly situated persons as that of petitioner, shall take a
decision in accordance with law within 90 days from the date of
receiving of the representation alongwith the order of this Court.

entire dues shall be paid within 30 days thereafter.
Accordingly, the present writ petition is disposed off.
