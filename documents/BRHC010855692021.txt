application for removing the encroachment from the public road

Madhubani.

Siddiqui, AC to SC-25 that in compliance of order dated
24.11.2022, the Encroachment Case No. 06 of 2022 was

show cause as to why the encroachment from the land in

document produced by respondent no. 6. The encroachment was
finally removed from the land in question on 14.12.2022.

brought on record the order dated 24.11.2022 informing this
Court that the encroachment has already been removed. Counter
affidavit is accepted.
In above view of the facts and circumstances, the
present writ petition is consigned.
