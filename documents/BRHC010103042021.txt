ORAL ORDER
2 02-01-2023   Learned Counsel for the petitioner submits that there
is private dispute between the petitioner and respondent nos. 7
to 10 inasmuch as the petitioner is the bona fide purchaser of the
land in question, but respondent nos. 7 to 10 have encroached
upon his land and are making construction over the same.


Patna High Court CWJC No.5754 of 2021(2) dt.02-01-2023
2/2 
In view of the fact that this case pertains to private
dispute, I am not inclined to entertain this writ application.
This application is, accordingly, dismissed.
However, the petitioner, if so advised, may file a suit
before the competent Court/authority for redressal of his
grievance.
