2 03-01-2023 The petitioner has an alternative remedy of filing an
appeal  in  terms  of  the  Rules  of  the  Bihar  State
Teaching  Institutions  Teachers  and  Employees  (Dispute
Redressal & Appeal) Rules 2020.  In  the judgment  dated
23.02.2022 passed in C.W.J.C. No. 5489 of 2020 (Suresh Ram
Vrs. The State of Bihar & Ors.) and connected matters reported


Patna High Court CWJC No.17644 of 2022(2) dt.03-01-2023
2/2 
in 2022 (2) B.L.J., 381  and the judgment  dated 07.03.2022
passed in  C.W.J.C. No.  34 of 2022 (Minakshi @ Sushre
Minakshi & Anr. Vrs. The State of Bihar & Ors.), this Court also
held that the writ could not be maintainable in view of the
alternative remedy available to the petitioner.
Granting liberty to the petitioner to file remedy as per
Rules of the  Bihar State Teaching Institutions Teachers and
Employees (Dispute Redressal & Appeal) Rules 2020 , the writ
petition is dismissed.
