ORAL ORDER
(Per: HONOURABLE MR. JUSTICE ASHUTOSH KUMAR)
3 03-01-2023 Heard Mr. Vijay Anand, learned Advocate for the
petitioner and Mr. Upendra Pratap Singh, learned A.C. to
S.C.-4 for the State.   
The petitioner is aggrieved by the grant of licence to
respondent no. 6 when the claim of the petitioner, according
to him, was the best amongst seekers. 
Several grounds have been raised on behalf of the
petitioner including that the petitioner was not given any
weightage under the Disability Act, to which he was entitled. 
In view of the nature of controversy raised and in


Patna High Court CWJC No.21778 of 2018(3) dt.03-01-2023
2/2 
view of the notification dated 21.07.2022 issued by the
Government in exercise of the powers conferred under
Sections 3 and 5 of the Essential Commodities Act, 1955 read
with Clause-36 of the Bihar Targeted Public Distribution
System (Control) Order, 2016, we direct that in the event of
the petitioner making a suitable representation/complaint
before the concerned Divisional Commissioner within a
period of 30 days, the Divisional Commissioner shall look
into the matter and after hearing all the stakeholders,
including respondent No.6, shall pass a final order within next
60 days, giving reasons in support of the decision taken by
him.
We have specifically asked for respondent no.6 to
be heard by the Divisional Commissioner for the reason that
this order has been passed in his absence.
With the afore-noted direction, the writ petition
stands disposed off.
