3 04-01-2023   Petitioner has prayed for the following relief(s):
“For issuance of an appropriate writ
in the nature of certiorari for quashing the order
dated 30.7.l2019 passed by Certificate Officer,
Banka passed in Certificate Case No. 56/2014-
15 contained in Annexure-3 to this petition, by
which a B/W was issued against the petitioner
due  to  non  deposit  of  remaining  certificate
amount without considering the facts that as per
Hon’ble Patna High Court’s direction passed in


Patna High Court CWJC No.24235 of 2019(3) dt.04-01-2023
2/2 
Cr. Misc. No. 21643/2015 on 15.05.2015, the
petitioner is requires to deposit 50% of his
salary  towards  the  repayment  of  certificate
amount but the petitioner has not paid his salary
regularly month to month basis and after a gap
of 4 to 6 months, when the petitioner received
salary, he is depositing the amount and is ready
to comply the same.”
On 02.01.2023, we had passed the following order:
“Despite repeated calls, none has
entered appearance on behalf of the petitioner.
However in the interest of justice matter is
adjourned.
List on 04.01.2023 in the category
of ‘Order Matters’.”
Even today, despite repeated calls and pass over,
none appears for the petitioner.
Petition is dismissed for default.
