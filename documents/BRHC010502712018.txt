CORAM: HONOURABLE MR. JUSTICE PRABHAT KUMAR SINGH
ORAL ORDER
5 03-01-2023 Heard learned counsel for the petitioner and the State.
Pursuant to the order of this Court dated 22.7.2017
(Annexure 12), passed in LPA No. 49 of 2017,  petitioner had
preferred appeal before the the Appellate Authority, i.e., the
Secretary, Rural Works Department, Government of Bihar


Patna High Court CWJC No.905 of 2018(5) dt.03-01-2023
2/3 
(respondent no.1) which was dismissed vide order dated
18.12.2017 (Annexure 13). 
From perusal of the memo of appeal, it appears that
the petitioner has not made out any ground as to how his case is
similar to that of his senior officer, namely, Ram Bilash Sharma,
who was awarded lesser punishment. Defence of the  petitioner
is that since his senior officer has been awarded lesser
punishment, therefore, he should also be given similar
treatment. 
While dismissing the appeal, the Appellate Authority
has stated that at the relevant time the petitioner was working as
Junior Engineer in the department and he has been found guilty
of excess entry of the work. It has been observed that being
Junior Engineer it was the duty of the  petitioner to enter correct
measurement of the work done in the measurement book. The
Appellate Authority has specifically held that the entry of work
in question in the measurement book is the main responsibility
of the  petitioner and not of his senior officer, as such, severe
punishment awarded to him is justified. 
Considering the fact that the  petitioner has not made
out a case showing similarity with the case of his senior officer
who has been awarded lesser punishment as also the fact that


Patna High Court CWJC No.905 of 2018(5) dt.03-01-2023
3/3 
the Appellate Authority has given ample reasoning for
upholding the punishment order, I do  not find any merit in the
writ petition. It is dismissed accordingly.
