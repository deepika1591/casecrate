ORAL ORDER
3 03-01-2023   The  grievance  of  the  petitioner  is  that  the
demarcation case was initiated in 2016-17 vide Measurement
Case  No.  11/16-17  by  the  Circle  Officer,  Fatuha  for
measurement of the land situated in Plot No. 216, Khata No.
123, Thana No. 23 having an area 11 Decimal of Mauza Raipura
within the Block of Fatuha, District-Patna.
The contention of the petitioner is that for the last
many years the demarcation case has not yet been decided. 
Learned counsel for the State submits that Circle
Officer may be directed to dispose the demarcation case within
a reasonable time frame. 
Taking  into  consideration  the  fact  that  the


Patna High Court CWJC No.14424 of 2018(3) dt.03-01-2023
2/2 
demarcation case is pending for the last five-six years, the
Circle Officer, Fatuha is directed to dispose the Measurement
Case No. 11/16-17 within a period of two months positively. 
With the aforesaid observation and direction, the
petition stands disposed of.
