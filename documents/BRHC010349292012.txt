ORAL ORDER
2 02-01-2023 1.  The  petitioner  by  way  of  this  writ  petition  has
prayed as under:-
“That this writ application is being filed for the
following reliefs:-
I) For issuance of an appropriate writ/ order/
direction for directing the respondents to make appointment
of the petitioner on the vacant tenure post of senior
Resident/ Tutor at the Medical College and Hospital of the
State under the provision of Bihar Medical Education
Service Cadre Recruitment, Appointment and Promotion
Rules, 2008.
II) For issuance of an appropriate writ/ order/


Patna High Court CWJC No.23596 of 2012(2) dt.02-01-2023
2/2 
direction for any other relief/ reliefs for which the petitioner
is entitled.”
2. Admittedly, the post was a tenure post for which
advertisement was issued in 2009. The post of Senior Resident/
Tutor being a tenure post was temporary in nature and therefore
cannot be now directed to be filled by the petitioner after lapse
of almost twelve years.
3. The writ petition is by efflux of time has become
redundant and is accordingly dismissed.
