 2. Petitioner had approached this Court for seeking
regularization of his services as a Sweeper. However, learned
counsel appearing for the University informed that the Principal
has terminated his services from 02.01.2019 after the notices
were served upon the Principal of the present writ petition.
3. Learned counsel appearing for the petitioner
submits that the action was vindictive and with the purpose to
deny the petitioner’s regularization of service. While it may be
true, this Court would not be able to pass any orders of
regularization at this stage as his services has already been
