From bare perusal of the above two government
Letter No. 739 and government resolution contained in  Memo
No. 01/ PRa Aa (COVID-19)-09/2021 dated 09.06.2021 it


Patna High Court CWJC No.1283 of 2022(3) dt.03-01-2023
5/6 
appears that the State Government has come out with a policy in
confirmity with the Central Government policy to compensate
all the persons who were treated for COVID-19 and had died in
course of treatment, their family should be compensated a sum
of Rs. 4 lacs by the State Government and Rs. 50,000/- by the
Central Government.
In spite of such clear cut condition laid down in the
two government decisions, the claim of the petitioner, whose
husband had died due to COVID-19 has been rejected on the
flimsy ground that the petitioner could not made available
RTPCR test positive report before the three member team. The
reason given in for rejection is not acceptable in the given facts
of the case. The petitioner has brought on record several
prescription and the reports which were performed at
government recognized centre and the husband of the petitioner
was treated at Sadar Hospital, Saran at Chapra confirms the fact
that the husband of the petitioner was treated for COVID-19
symptoms and ultimately while he was taken to P.M.C.H.,
Patna, he died enroute.
At this stage, District Magistrate, Saran at Chapra
submitted that he will reconstitute a team of doctors at his level
to confirm the report and will not go into technicality that the


Patna High Court CWJC No.1283 of 2022(3) dt.03-01-2023
6/6 
RTPRC test has not been performed at Government approved
laboratory. This Court appreciates the submission made by
learned District Magistrate, Saran at Chapra and it is expected
from him that the claim of the petitioner will be true spirit of the
beneficial government announcement. 
This Court directs the District Magistrate, Saran at
Chapra that he must not take any technical impediment. In case
he needs any clarification then in that case he may refer the
matter to the Additional Chief Secretary, Health Department for
considering the case of the petitioner for payment of
compensation.
Such exercise must be carried out within three week
by the District Magistrate, Saran at Chapra. In case matter is
referred by him to the Additional Chief Secretary, Health
Department in that case the Additional Chief Secretary, Health
Department is directed to redress the claim of the petitioner
forthwith within a period of two weeks.
With above observation, the present writ petition is
disposed of.
