Heard  the  learned  counsel  for  the
parties.
2. The petitioner has come up with a


Patna High Court CWJC No.6788 of 2019 dt.02-01-2023
2/3 
queer prayer that the beneficiaries who have been
detached from the shop of the petitioner be again
tagged with his shop as his shop is left with lesser
number  of  targeted  beneficiaries,  which  inures
neither  in  favour  of  the  petitioner  nor  the
beneficiaries.
3.  In  any  view  of  the  matter,  the
petitioner has not been able to make out any case
for us to interfere in this jurisdiction for the reason
that the central focus of the Bihar Targeted Public
Distribution System (Control) Order, 2016 is not
the licensee, but the targeted beneficiaries and we
do  not  find  any  complaint  by  any  one  of  the
targeted beneficiaries who have been attached with
another P.D.S. shop.
4.  Nonetheless,  the  counsel  for  the
petitioner, after some arguments, withdraws this
petition in order to, if so desired, agitate her cause
before the licensing authority.


Patna High Court CWJC No.6788 of 2019 dt.02-01-2023
3/3 
5. Disposed off as withdrawn with the
liberty aforesaid.
