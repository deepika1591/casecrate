Petitioners have prayed for the following relief(s):- 
i. To declare Rule 7 of 2014 Rules by
which the scale of Rs.5200- 20,200 with grade
pay Rs. 2800 of Agriculture Co-ordinator has
arbitrarily fixed lower than their other counter
parts, similar qualified and discharging similar
functions  and  further  direct  to  issue  an
appropriate writ(s) order(s)/ direction(s) in the
nature  of  mandamus commanding  the
respondents to amend Rule 7 of Agriculture Co-
ordinator  Cadre  (Recruitment  &  Service
Condition) Rules, 2014 and grant a pay scale of
Rs. 9300-34800 with grade pay of Rs.5400 in
place of pay scale of Rs.5200- 20,200 with
grade pay Rs. 2800 in exercise of power
conferred under Rule 3(b) of the Rules.
ii. To declare and hold that the degree/certificate
possessing by the petitioners are technical
degree and entitled to a pay scale of Rs.9300-
34800 with grade pay of Rs.5400/- which has

been fixed in respect of the pay scale fixed for
the appointments made on the basis of the
technical degree.
iii. To issue an appropriate writ(s) order(s)
direction(s)  in  the  nature  of  mandamus
commanding the respondent to amend Rule 7
and grant the pay scale of 9300-34800 Grade
pay of 5400/-. On the principle of equal pay for
equal work/qualification.
iv. To issue an appropriate writ(s) order(s)/
direction(s)  in  the  nature  of  mandamus
commanding the respondents to grant the
petitioner equivalent pay scale of B.VSC or
B.VSc & AH.
v. To any other relief(s) that the petitioners are
entitled to in the facts and of the case.”
Mr. Y.V Giri, learned Senior counsel appearing for
the petitioners, states that the petitioners shall be content if the
petition is disposed of with the request to respondent no. 4,
namely The Secretary, Agriculture Department, Government
of Bihar, Patna to consider and decide the petitioners’ request
similar in nature which the petitioner would be making as was
so made to respondent no. 3 through the communication dated
25.2.2019 (Annexure 12, Page 89) and analogous request.
State has no objection to the same.
As such, petition is disposed of in the following
mutually agreeable terms:
