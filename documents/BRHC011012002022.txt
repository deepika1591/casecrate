ORAL ORDER
2 03-01-2023 Heard the parties.
The petitioner by way of this writ petition prays for
directing the District Teachers Appointment Appellate Authority,
Siwan, to dispose of the appeal of the petitioner.
In the opinion of this Court, day-to-day functioning of
the judicial/quasi judicial authorities ought not be interfered by
this Court by exercising jurisdiction under Article 226 of the
Constitution of India.  Each Court has method of working and


Patna High Court CWJC No.17744 of 2022(2) dt.03-01-2023
2/2 
taking-up of cases has to be exclusively as per the procedure
laid down.  If any person wants case to be listed out of turn,
appropriate application can be moved in that judicial forum for
the said purpose.  The writ petition would not lie as this Court
can not be converted into a Court of supervision and hearing
early listing application.
The writ petition is misconceived and is, accordingly,
dismissed.
