4 04-01-2023   Heard learned counsel for the petitioner, counsel for
the Respondents and  counsel for A.G., Bihar.
The present Writ application has been filed for
seeking direction to make payment of present retiral dues with
interest vide  PPO No. - 434631. The petitioner has retired on
31.07.2005 as an Assistant Teacher from Government High
School, Bihar.
Learned counsel for the petitioner submits that during

Patna High Court CWJC No.14281 of 2021(4) dt.04-01-2023
2/2 
the pendency of the Writ application, authority letter has been
issued but no payment has been made till date.
The respondent no. 3 is directed to assure the payment
of the petitioner within 60 days of filing of representation along
with the copy of entire brief, who shall assure payment within
60 days from the date of filing of the representation of the
petitioner. 
If payment shall not be made within the said period, it
is directed that respondent shall pay 9% interest on the said
amount.
With this direction, the present Writ petition is
disposed off.
