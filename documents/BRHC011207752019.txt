      Petitioner has prayed for the following relief(s):-
“(i) For issuance of an appropriate writ writs, order
orders,  direction\  directions  in  the  nature  of
certiorari for quashing the Award No. IOIPATIALI
0055\2019-2020  dated  12.09.2019(which  was
communicated to the petitioner through letter dated
16.09 2019) passed by the Insurance Ombudsman
respondent  no.6  whereby  he  dismissed  the


Patna High Court CWJC No.23681 of 2019 dt.02-01-2023
2/3 
complaint filed by petitioner for claim relates to
Double Accident Benefit in short" DAB) under
Policy No 519701916 to the life of Late Ghan
Shyam Tiwari and affirmed the orders passed by
respondent no.2 and 3.
(ii) For issuance of an appropriate writh writs,
order/orders, direction! directions in the nature of
certiorari for quashing of order dated 1.12.2017
passed  by  Respondent  no.3  and  order  dated
31.3.2018 passed by respondent no 2 whereby he
affirmed the order dated 1.12.2017 passed by
respondent no.3
(iii) For issuance of an appropriate writ writs,
order/orders, direction directions in the nature of
mandamus directing the respondent authorities to
pay the benefits of Double Accident Benefits to the
petitioner under the policy No. 519701916 to the
life of Late Ghan Shyam Tiwari. 
(iv) For grant of any relief(s) the petitioner would
be found entitled to in the facts and circumstances
of the case.
              The order dated 12.09.2019 (Page-19) passed by the
Respondent No. 6, namely, the Insurance Ombudsman, State of
Bihar and Jharkhand, Kappana Arcade, First Floor, Bazar Samiti
Road, Bahadurpur, Patna needs to be quashed and set aside for
the reason that neither the petitioner was heard nor informed of
the date on which the proceedings were to be heard and decided.
Notice of such proceeding was received by the petitioner on


Patna High Court CWJC No.23681 of 2019 dt.02-01-2023
3/3 
19.9.2019 whereas the order was passed on 12.9.2019.
As such, on this ground alone, we quash and set
aside the order dated 12.09.2019 (Page-19) passed by the
Respondent No. 6, namely, the Insurance Ombudsman, State of
Bihar and Jharkhand, Kappana Arcade, First Floor, Bazar Samiti
Road, Bahadurpur, Patna. 
The  petitioner  shall  appear  before  the
Respondent No. 6, namely, the Insurance Ombudsman, State of
Bihar and Jharkhand, Kappana Arcade, First Floor, Bazar Samiti
Road, Bahadurpur, Patna, on 16.01.2023 at 10:30 A.M., who
shall, after hearing the parties, pass a fresh order in accordance
with law. 
Petition stands disposed of.
Interlocutory application, if any, shall also stand
disposed of.
