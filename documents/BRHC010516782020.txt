01.02.2006  to  31.07.2007,  23.10.2007  to  05.11.2007,
02.05.2008  to  08.05.2008,  16.05.2008  to  20.05.2008  and
03.06.2008 to 20.06.2008 and further to direct the respondent
no.8 DPFO Jehanabad to issue a authority slip for payment of
G.P.F. to the petitioner. 
Learned counsel for the State has appeared and filed
his counter affidavit on behalf of the respondent nos.5 to 11 as
well as the counter affidavit filed by the respondent nos.4 to 8
by DPFO, Jehanabad . 
Admittedly the present writ petition has been filed by
the wife of the employee. According to her, her husband was
appointed on 15.05.2000 and died on 18.11.2014, after death the
P.P.O. and G.P.O. has been issued.
Counsel for the petitioner submits that her husband
did not receive the dues payment of salary and GPF amount. In
this regard the series of correspondence were made between the
officials. Lastly the petitioner has represented on 14.08.2020 for
payment of legal dues which has not been paid to her. There are
series of letters and reply attached in this writ petition by way of
counter affidavit also. 
For the purpose of deciding this disputes the official
records are required.

3/3 
In this view of the matter, the District Magistrate-
cum-Collector, Arwal district is directed to resolve the present
issue within period of 60 days from the date of receiving of the
representation by the petitioner, considering all the documents
annexed in the writ petition which shall be placed before him by
the petitioner. It is also directed to the District Magistrate-cum-
Collector, Arwal district that in case of admitted dues he shall
assure payment within 60 days thereafter. 
With this direction, the writ petition is disposed off.
