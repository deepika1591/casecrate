ORAL ORDER
10 02-01-2023 Let the defect(s), if any, be removed within two weeks
from today.  
Heard learned counsel for the petitioner and learned
counsel for the respondent.
The petitioner has filed the present writ petition for
payment of balance of arrears of pension. The following reliefs
for this present writ application are:-
I. For payment of balance of arrears of pension from
the date of entitlement in view of Resolution No.755 dated
20.10.2017 of Finance Department, Govt, of Bihar, Patna, as per
which Pension/Family Pension of Govt. Employees retired prior
to 01.01.2016 has to be given Pension/Family Pension at revised
rate after refixing the same.

Patna High Court CWJC No.2388 of 2021(10) dt.02-01-2023
2/3 
II. For issuance of a writ in the nature of Mandamus
commanding/directing the Respondents to restore all retirement
benefits to the petitioner, who superannuated on 31.01.2002
after completing the service of 33 years 6 months 12 days, as
per the pay fixation on the basis of the office order no 316 of
2001 issued by Respondent Authority vide memo no 445/2001
dated 07.02.2001 whereby and whereunder pay scale of
petitioner was revised to Rs.8650.
III. For issuance of a writ in the nature of mandamus
directing the Respondents to fix the pension as per the pay
fixation recommended in District Order No. 316/2001 passed by
superintended of Police, Muzzafarpur, Bihar vide memo no 445
dated 07.02.2001 and pay the arrears of the difference of
amount with interest as till today as the petitioner is getting the
pension less in terms of last pay fixation.
IV. To issue any other writ, order or direction which
this Hon’ble Court may deem, fit and proper under the facts and
circumstances of the case.
Pleadings in this case has already been completed.
Admittedly, the petitioner was appointed on the post of
Constable on 20.07.1968 and superannuated from the service on
31.01.2002 as Sub-inspector. The petitioner has only grievance

Patna High Court CWJC No.2388 of 2021(10) dt.02-01-2023
3/3 
that he was entitled for pay scale of Rs. 8,650/- with effect from
01.01.2021 instead of  Rs. 8475. In this regard, the petitioner
has filed series of representations for the respondent No.3 who
is the competent person to pass order in the said matter.
Pleading has been completed but court is of the view that a
competent person ought to have passed the order.
In this view of the matter, let the respondent No.3 is
directed to pass order. All the representation filed by the
petitioner considering all the pleadings placed before this court
within 60 days from the date of receiving the order passed by
this court.
With this direction, this writ application is disposed
off. It is made clear that the Superintendent of Police(respondent
No.3) is free to pass order in accordance with law without
prejudice from the order passed by this court.
