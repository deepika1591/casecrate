behalf of the petitioner, Mr. Mukesh Kumar, learned counsel for
the Bihar State Election Authority and Mr. Narendra Kumar,
learned AC to GP 22.
2. The present writ application has been filed for the
following reliefs:
(i) To issue an appropriate writ preferably in the
nature of certiorari  for  setting aside the order
dated 27.11.2017 bearing memo no. 1169, dated
28.11.2017,  passed  by  the  Chief  Electoral
Officer,  Bihar  State  Election  Authority,  Patna
whereby and whereunder the application made
by  the  petitioner  for  exclusion  of  names  of
respondents  5  to  10  from  the  voters  list  of
Aurangabad  Vyapar  Mandal  Co-operative
Society Ltd. Published on the basis of cut off
date 31.08.2017 has been dismissed;
(ii)  To  hold  and  declare  that  the  names  of
respondent No. 5 to 10 were illegally included in
the  voters  list  prepared  and  published  for  the
year, 2012;
(iii) To hold and declare that the respondent no.
5 to 10 does not have the legal entitlement to be
included  as  voters  since  they  are  neither
members nor share holders on the co-operative
Society, and
(iv) To grant any other relief(s), for which the
petitioner  may  be  found  entitled  to  in  the
peculiar facts and circumstances of the case.
3. Mr. Mukesh Kumar, learned counsel for the Bihar
State Election Authority submits that so far the reliefs prayed for
in  the  present  writ  application  is  concerned,  the  same  has
become  infructuous,   in  view  of  the  fact  that  during  the
pendency  of  the  present  writ  application,  the  Election  has


Patna High Court CWJC No.19367 of 2017(2) dt.02-01-2023
3/3 
already  held. He further submits that the petitioner has only
grievance with regard to the inclusion of the name of private
respondent, for which the petitioner has a remedy before the
Registrar, Co-operative Society in view of Section 48 of the
Bihar Co-operative Societies Act, 1935. 
4.  Faced  with  the  situation,  learned  senior  counsel
appearing  on  behalf  of  the  petitioner  submits  that  the  writ
application may be disposed of with a liberty to the petitioner to
avail the remedy available under the Statute.
5. In view of the submissions made on behalf of the
parties, the present writ application stands disposed off with a
liberty to the petitioner to avail the remedy, as provided under
the Statute.
6. It is needless to say that if such application is filed
before the Registrar Co-operative Society, the same would be
considered and disposed off expeditiously, in accordance with
law.
