CORAM: HONOURABLE MR. JUSTICE ANIL KUMAR SINHA
ORAL ORDER
2 02-01-2023 Land of the petitioners bearing Khata No. 578,
Khesra No. 1847 in Thana No. 161,  Mauza – Nadwan has
been acquired by the National Highway Authority of India
(for short “N.H.A.I.).
The  grievance  of  the  petitioners   is  that
compensation of the subject land acquired by the N.H.A.I.
is not  adequate and for enhancement of the same the


Patna High Court CWJC No.6357 of 2021(2) dt.02-01-2023
2/3 
petitioners have filed representation before the District
Collector, Patna.
Mr. S.N. Pathak, learned counsel appearing for the
N.H.A.I. submits that as per the Section 3-G(5) of the
National Highways Act, 1956 (hereinafter referred to as
“N.H. Act”) “if the amount determined by  the competent
authority under sub-section (1) or sub-section (2) is not
acceptable to either of the parties, the amount shall, on an
application by either of the parties, be determined by the
Arbitrator to be appointed by Central Government”. He
next submits that now the Central Government has  notified
Divisional Commissioner  of the area as Arbitrator under
the N.H. Act.
In view of the aforesaid, the petitioners are
directed to  file  their  claim  for  enhancement of  the
compensation amount as per Section 3-G(5) of the N.H. Act
before the Arbitrator i.e. Divisional Commissioner, Patna
within a period of four weeks from today. If such a claim is
filed within the aforesaid time, the learned Arbitrator shall
decide the same in accordance with law giving opportunity
of hearing to all concerned and will pass a reasoned order


Patna High Court CWJC No.6357 of 2021(2) dt.02-01-2023
3/3 
within a period of six months thereafter.
With the aforesaid observation and direction, this
writ application is disposed of.
    
praful/-
