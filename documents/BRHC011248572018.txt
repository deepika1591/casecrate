ORAL ORDER
(Per: HONOURABLE MR. JUSTICE ASHUTOSH KUMAR)
2 02-01-2023 Heard the learned counsel for the parties. 
Mr. Dhananjaya Nath Tiwari, the learned Advocate
for the petitioner submits that the application for grant of licence
on compassionate ground of the petitioner is still pending before
the authorities. 
There is no reason why such applications should
remain pending before the authorities for such long time. 
Believing the statement of the petitioner to be true, we
direct that in case the application of the petitioner is pending for

Patna High Court CWJC No.23362 of 2018(2) dt.02-01-2023
2/2 
consideration for grant of licence on compassionate ground, the
same shall be taken up by the Authority/District Selection
Committee within next 60 days and an order shall be passed
either allowing or disallowing the claim of the petitioner in
accordance with law. The petitioner shall be made known the
outcome of such decision by the Committee.
The petition stands disposed off accordingly.
