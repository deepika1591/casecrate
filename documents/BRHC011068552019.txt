Patna High Court CWJC No.22780 of 2019(2) dt.03-01-2023
2/3 
In view of the disputed question of fact so raised
by the respondents, learned counsel for the petitioner seeks
permission to withdraw the present petition reserving
liberty to either approach the respondents pointing out the
relevant facts necessary for adjudicating the petitioner’s
claim and/or initiate appropriate  proceedings  before
different foras in accordance with law. 
Liberty as prayed for is granted. 
We are sure that as and when any such
proceedings  are  initiated,  before  the  appropriate
forum/Tribunal, the same shall be considered and decided
expeditiously, in accordance with law.
Needless to add, period for which the petitioner
has been pursuing the matter before this Court, shall be
excluded for the purpose of computing limitation.  
It is made clear that we have not expressed any
opinion on merits. All issues are left open. 
The petition stands disposed of as withdrawn
with the liberty aforesaid.
