ORAL ORDER
3 02-01-2023 Heard learned counsel for the petitioner and the State.
This  writ petition has been filed for commanding
respondents no. 3 and 4 to provide protection of life and
property to the  petitioner caused due to atrocity of private
respondents no. 8 to 13.  
Counter  affidavit  has  been  filed  on  behalf  of


Patna High Court CWJC No.13619 of 2015(3) dt.02-01-2023
2/2 
respondents no. 2, 3, 5 and 6. It has been stated that there is land
dispute between the  petitioner and private respondents and
they are fighting for getting right, title and possession over the
said land. It is further stated that proceedings under sections 107
and 144 of the Cr.P.C. have also been drawn between the parties
to maintain peace and tranquility. Learned counsel for the State
submits that it is a case of civil dispute between the  petitioner
and the private respondents and the  petitioner has alternative
remedy.
It appears that the State officials have already taken
proper steps to refrain the parties from committing any overt act
in the matter, no further order is required to be passed. 
Writ petition is disposed of as such.
