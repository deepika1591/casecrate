2 02-01-2023 Heard the learned counsel for the parties.   
Mr.  Dhananjay  Nath  Tiwary,  the  learned
Advocate for the petitioner submits that even though
money for the food grains for the months of September
and October, 2013 was paid by him in advance, no food


Patna High Court CWJC No.5539 of 2019(2) dt.02-01-2023
2/3 
grains were supplied.
Despite persistent request by the petitioner, a
licensee under the Public Distribution System, for either
return of the amount or its adjustment for the succeeding
months, no decision was taken.
When all the efforts failed and considerable time
had elapsed, the petitioner has preferred the instant writ
petition with the alternative prayer of either securing a
direction to the authorities to return the money or its
adjustment for the succeeding months. 
Considering the afore-noted submission of the
petitioner, we direct the District Magistrate to look into the
matter and if at all no supply was made for the months of
September and October, 2013 for which money had been
accepted from the petitioner, necessary sequel order shall
be passed but only after ascertaining the correctness of
the statements so made in the writ petition.
A decision in that regard shall be taken by the
Collector within a period of 90 days of the production of a
copy of this order and an application along with the


Patna High Court CWJC No.5539 of 2019(2) dt.02-01-2023
3/3 
aforesaid order by the petitioner.
With the afore-noted direction/observation, the
petition stands disposed off.
