2 03-01-2023 1. Even in the second round, no one appears on behalf
of the petitioner.
2.  The  petitioner  by  way  of  this  writ  petition
challenges the letter dated 23.06.2022, whereby the petitioner
was informed under Right to Information Act that her Centre
was cancelled. Letter issued under Right to Information cannot
be made as a subject matter of challenge in writ petition as it is


Patna High Court CWJC No.17731 of 2022(2) dt.03-01-2023
2/2 
only an information and not an order taking away any right of
individual.
3. The writ petition is wholly mis-conceived and is
accordingly dismissed.
