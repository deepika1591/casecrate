2 03-01-2023   Petitioner has prayed for the following relief (s) :- 
“(I) For issuance of a writ in the nature of
certiorari  for  quashing  part  of  the  information
uploaded  by  the  respondent  Inland  Waterways.
Authority of India (hereinafter referred to as the
Authority for short) on the official website of the said
authority on 20.11.2019 whereby the petitioner has
been intimated about rejection of technical bid and
declared as technically disqualified without assigning
any reason
(ii) For issuance of a writ or order or direction
in  the  nature  of  mandamus  commanding  the
respondent authority to treat the technical bid of the

petitioner as valid and also to open the financial bid of
the petitioner for the purpose of consideration for

(iii) For holding and a declaration that in
absence  of  any response  from the  ends  of the
respondent bank as regards the verification of the
solvency certificate submitted by the petitioner the
respondent authority could not have presumed the

not  have  held  the  petitioner  disqualified  in  the
technical bid evaluation;
(iv) For holding and a declaration that the
issue of confirmation of solvency certificate being not
one of the eligibility criteria prescribed in the tender
document  could  not  have  formed  the  basis  of
disqualification of the petitioner in the technical bid
evaluation process;
(v) For grant of any other relief(s) to which the
petitioner  is  found  entitled  to  in  the  facts  and
circumstances of the present case.”
Learned counsel for the petitioner seeks permission to
withdraw the present petition. 
Permission granted. 
Present petition stands disposed of as withdrawn.
