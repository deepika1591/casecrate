CORAM: HONOURABLE MR. JUSTICE DR. ANSHUMAN
ORAL ORDER
3 02-01-2023  The learned counsel for the petitioner is directed to
add Patna Commissioner as respondent no. 6 in this case who is
the competent authority to decide issue involved in the present
case.
Heard learned counsel for the petitioner and learned
counsel for the Respondents.
The present Writ Petition has been filed directing the
respondent to make payment of retrial dues including Earned
Leave, payment of arrear grant of A.C.P., arrear of salary as per

Patna High Court CWJC No.13022 of 2021(3) dt.02-01-2023
2/3 
the 6th and 7th Pay Commission and accordingly for fixation and
payment of monthly pension.
At the very outset learned counsel for the petitioner
submits that the petitioner has joined service as Lady Supervisor
in the I.C.D.S. Government of Bihar, Patna on 13.07.1986 and
finally she retired from service as Lady Supervisor working
under Child Development Programme Officer, Danapur, Patna.
In this way she claimed herself to serve for about 32 years and 8
months.
Learned  counsel  for  the  petitioner  submits  that
petitioner has filed a representation before Patna Commissioner
which is Annexure-2 on 29.12.2020 but there is no response on
the said representation till date.
Learned counsel for the State submits that counter
affidavit has already been filed with two annexures, in which it
has been stated that the petitioner has also moved before office
of Lokayukta. According to learned counsel for the petitioner
the said matter has already been ended but till date petitioner
has not received any payment.
In this view of the matter, let the respondent no. 6 is
directed to take a decision. Let the Commissioner, Patna
Commissionary is directed to consider the entire matter of the

Patna High Court CWJC No.13022 of 2021(3) dt.02-01-2023
3/3 
petitioner upon filing a fresh representation along with copy of
the Writ Petition and decide the claim of petitioner within 90
days of receiving the fresh representation. The Commissioner
shall also assure the payment to the petitioner within 30 days
thereafter.
With this direction the present application is disposed
off.
