ORAL ORDER
2 02-01-2023 Heard the parties.
The petitioner assails the action of the respondents in
not calling him for interview and also challenges the order of
University in declaring the petitioner ineligible stating that the
petitioner  had  submitted  his  documents  and  hard  copy  by
sending them through speed post on 29.12.2020.  However, the
candidature has been rejected wrongfully.
I have considered the submissions.
While the petitioner has mentioned about having sent
the hard copy on 29.12.2020, by speed post, he has not been


Patna High Court CWJC No.17516 of 2022(2) dt.02-01-2023
2/2 
able to prove that the speed post was duly received by the
respondent-Commission before 30 th of December, 2020.  In the
absence of any such documentary evidence on record, this Court
can not conclude on the aspects whether the application has
been received or not before the cut-off date.
In  view  of  specific  averments  in  the  order  dated
18.11.2022 issued by the Commission, a presumption has to be
drawn that the hard copy of the application was not received in
the office of  the Commission  and he was therefore rightly
declared ineligible.
In  view  thereof,  the  writ  petition  is  wholly
misconceived and is dismissed.
