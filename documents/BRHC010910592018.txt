ORAL ORDER
14 02-01-2023 In the second supplementary counter-affidavit filed on
behalf of respondent Nos. 1 to 3, it is stated in paragraph 5 that
the concerned University through its Registrar vide letter dated
10.11.2022 has intimated that both B.Sc. (CA & BM) and B.Sc.
(Ag.) degrees are equivalent to qualify for the technical
requirement for selections.  In paragraph 6 it is stated that in the
light of aforesaid facts now necessary further steps shall be
taken by the respondent authorities. 
In view of the aforesaid facts and circumstances, this
writ petition is disposed of with direction to the respondent
