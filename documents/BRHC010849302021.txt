ORAL ORDER
3 03-01-2023   Heard the parties. 
 It is informed by learned counsel appearing on behalf
of the petitioner that some amount of compensation has already
been released to the  petitioner under Scheduled Caste /
Scheduled Tribes (Prevention of Atrocities) Act , however
remaining amount has not been released so far, hence the
present writ petition. 
Learned counsel appearing on behalf of the State
submitted that he has received instruction that the criminal case


Patna High Court CWJC No.20571 of 2021(3) dt.03-01-2023
2/2 
lodged by the petitioner against the accused persons who were
involved in alleged murder of her husband is pending.
Considering the above submission made by the
parties, the District Magistrate, West Champaran is directed to
release the remaining amount of compensation in favour of the
petitioner if order of conviction has been passed in the criminal
case lodged by the petitioner against the accused persons. 
In case the criminal case lodged by the petitioner
against the accused persons is still pending, then in that case the
District  Magistrate,  Bettiah,  West  Champaran  and  the
Superintendent of Police, West Champaran must ensure to
produce all the prosecution witnesses for being examined in the
trial without fail on each and every date fixed by the trial court.  
With the aforesaid observation, the present writ
petition stands disposed of.
