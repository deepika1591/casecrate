CORAM: HONOURABLE MR. JUSTICE PRABHAT KUMAR SINGH
ORAL JUDGMENT
Date : 03-01-2023
Heard learned counsel appearing for the petitioners
as well as learned counsel for the State.
The present writ application has been filed on
behalf of petitioners for quashing the impugned order passed by
Assistant Director, DCPU, Aurangabad with further prayer to
restore the selection of the petitioners on the post of Social
Workers and to stop the written examination which would be


Patna High Court CWJC No.1621 of 2018 dt.03-01-2023
2/4 
held on 28.01.2018.
Brief facts of this case is that petitioners were
appointed earlier on contractual basis as Social Workers in
District Child Protection Unit, Aurangabad for a period of one
year. Pursuant to the same, petitioners entered into an agreement
with the concerned authority on 12.11.2012 and submitted their
joining on the same day. The period of contract was extended
from time to time for the period of four and half years. 
It is submitted on behalf of petitioners that at the
time of extension of period of contract the authorities concerned
submitted  bad  performance  appraisal  report  because  the
petitioners did not fulfill the illegal demand of gratification by
the authority. 
It is submitted that all the District Magistrates of
the State of Bihar were directed to submit Work Performance
Appraisal Report with regard to all the contractual employees
with name who were presently working in Child Protection
Unit, so that the work of their employment could be completed
in time. Taking into consideration the performance of the
petitioners the authorities submitted bad performance appraisal
report, as a result of which the services of the petitioners were
cancelled.


Patna High Court CWJC No.1621 of 2018 dt.03-01-2023
3/4 
On the other hand counsel for State by filing
counter  affidavit  submitted  that  throughout  the  period
petitioners were found deficient in their discharge of their
duties. These petitioners were found absent habitually after
making their attendance and in order to give chance to improve
their work performance the contract service period were
extended repeatedly with strict warning to improve their work
performance, but in spite of giving ample opportunities to the
petitioners,  no  improvement  were  found  in  their  work
performance  and  repeated  complain  of  disobedience  and
negligence in performance of their official duty was found, as a
result of which after considering all the facts and circumstances,
Assistant Director, District Child Protection Unit, Aurangabad
submitted work performance appraisal report to the District
Magistrate, Aurangabad, who being agreed with the report
found that the petitioners are not liable to be adjusted on the
post of Social Workers and accordingly, Performance Appraisal
Report of the petitioners were sent to the Director, Social
Welfare-cum-Vice Chairman, State Child Protection Committee,
Bihar,  Patna vide letter no. 122/DCPU dated 20.04.2017 of the
District Magistrate, Aurangabad. 
Considering the rival submissions of the parties and


Patna High Court CWJC No.1621 of 2018 dt.03-01-2023
4/4 
material available on record and the fact that thrice petitioners
were given warning, but petitioners did not improve their work
performance, I find no illegality and infirmity in the impugned
order passed by Assistant Director, DCPU, Aurangabad, as such,
this writ petition stands dismissed being devoid of merit.
