In view of the intervening developments, the scope
of the present petition is confined only with respect to the
cancellation of the petitioner’s registration, leaving it open for
the petitioner to agitate the grievances before the appropriate
forum in accordance with law.
Mr. Kumar Alok, learned Standing Counsel No.7,
states that petitioner has appropriate remedies in accordance
with law as and when such procedure shall be initiated the same
shall be decided in accordance with law.
At this stage, Mr. Kumar Alok, learned Standing
Counsel  No.7  states  that  insofar  as  the  surviving  issue  is
concerned, the petitioner can take recourse to the remedies
provided under section 11 of the Bihar Contract Registration
Rule, 2006 and, should the petitioner do so within next two
weeks,  the  appropriate  authority  shall  positively  decide  the
application, within four weeks thereafter.
Petition is disposed of accordingly with the liberty
aforesaid.
