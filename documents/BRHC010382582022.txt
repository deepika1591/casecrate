CORAM: HONOURABLE MR. JUSTICE PURNENDU SINGH
ORAL ORDER
2 02-01-2023 Heard Mr. Satish Kumar, l earned counsel appearing
on behalf of the petitioner  and Mr. Indeshwari Prasad Mandal,
learned AC to GA-3.
2. L earned counsel appearing on behalf of the
petitioner submitted that the  petitioner had entered into an
agreement with respondent no.7 for erection of mobile tower on
top floor of his house. The petitioner’s case is that as per the


Patna High Court CWJC No.7686 of 2022(2) dt.02-01-2023
2/3 
terms and conditions of the agreement, the mobile tower was
required to be removed within the prescribed period of 90 days
after issuance of notice and that having not been duly regarded
by respondent no.7, the petitioner was forced to approach the
District Magistrate, Bhagalpur. He further submitted that the
lessee is performing public duty by providing uninterrupted
mobile facility to the customer of Airtel. He further submitted
that he has made an application before the District Magistrate,
Bhagalpur for removal of the said tower and the same be
disposed of in accordance with law. 
3. Learned counsel appearing on behalf of the State
submitted that the writ petition is not maintainable. The
petitioner can only avail appropriate remedy before the Civil
Court for removal of the tower in accordance with law. 
4. Having considered the rival submissions of the
parties, though the dispute is relating to performance of the
either party as per the terms and conditions of the agreement
dated 15.07.2006, but taking into consideration the larger
interest of the public who have been provided with the mobile
connection which has become an essential service, this Court
directs the District Magistrate, Bhagalpur to take appropriate
legal action on the basis of the representation of the petitioner


Patna High Court CWJC No.7686 of 2022(2) dt.02-01-2023
3/3 
and if it is found that the building of the  petitioner is in
dilapidated condition which will cause harm to the surrounding
inhabitants as well as the petitioner along with other inhabitants
of the said house, the District Magistrate shall take appropriate
action in accordance with law. 
5. Accordingly, the writ petition stands disposed of.
