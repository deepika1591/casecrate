ORAL ORDER
6 02-01-2023 1.  Heard the parties. 
2.  The  petitioner  by  way  of  this  writ  petition
challenges  the  order  dated  31.12.1996,  whereby  he  was
removed from the post of clerk.
3. Learned counsel  for the petitioner  submits that
apart from him, no other person was removed from the post and
all  are  working  as  Clerk,  who  were  appointed  under  the
signature of Ram Yatan Paswan. It appears that there seems to
be some scam, whereby people were appointed wrongfully on
different post by one person and directions were issued by the
Director to  remove such illegally appointed persons. Apart from


Patna High Court CWJC No.1766 of 2018(6) dt.02-01-2023
2/2 
the fact that the writ petition has been filed after a period of
almost 22 years, this Court finds that the appointment made to
the petitioner was without conducting regular selection and in
an illegal manner.
4. Keeping in view thereto, the petitioner cannot claim
discrimination, as even others were appointed wrongfully.
5. The writ petition is found to be therefore wholly
misconceived, dismissed on the ground of latches, delay as well
as on merits.
