ORAL ORDER
2 02-01-2023 1. Learned counsel for petitioner submits that before
passing of the order impugned dated 27.09.2012, no opportunity
was given to the petitioner. It is stated that during the pendency
of this writ petition charge-sheet has already been filed in the
F.I.R. registered by the Respondents and the evidence has not
yet commenced in trial.
2.  Learned  counsel  submits  that  prior  notice/
opportunity of hearing was required to be given to the petitioner
before  terminating  her  services.  I  have  considered  the
submission. The petitioner was appointed on contract basis and


Patna High Court CWJC No.23567 of 2012(2) dt.02-01-2023
2/2 
if there is a doubt regarding the appointment having been
obtained by fraudulent means, no notice or opportunity of
hearing is required to be given at this stage. Moreover, charge-
sheet has been filed with regard to the F.I.R. lodged by the
Respondents. Keeping in view thereto, no relief can be granted
to the petitioner.
3.  The  writ  petition  is  mis-concieved  and  is
accordingly dismissed.
