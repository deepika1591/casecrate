ORAL ORDER
(Per: HONOURABLE MR. JUSTICE ASHUTOSH KUMAR)
2 02-01-2023   Heard Mr. Binay Kumar, the learned Advocate
for the petitioner and Mr. Upendra Pratap Singh, the
learned counsel for the State.
The licence of the petitioner was suspended
under Rule 28 of the Bihar Targeted Public Distribution
System (Control Order, 2016) for her having been made
accused in the criminal case involving breach of Essential
Commodities Act of 1955. 
The petitioner was given a show cause notice and


Patna High Court CWJC No.7650 of 2019(2) dt.02-01-2023
2/3 
was given three days’ time to respond to the notice to
which she did not reply.
No plausible reason has been assigned except for
a lesser window-time to her to represent her cause, but
according to the counsel representing the respondents, the
order of cancellation ultimately was passed much later.
Mr. Upendra Pratap Singh, the learned counsel
for the respondents submits that had the petitioner replied
even beyond the stipulated time, that could have been
considered, but her not having done so precludes her from
agitating the same cause before this Court.
After having heard the parties, we are of the view
that an opportunity ought to be given to the petitioner to
represent her cause.
For such reason, we, for the present, set-aside
the order cancelling the license of the petitioner and remit
the matter to the Licensing Authority to pass a fresh order
after adverting to the reply which shall be filed by the
petitioner within a period of 30 days from the date of
passing of this order. The Licensing Authority shall pass a


Patna High Court CWJC No.7650 of 2019(2) dt.02-01-2023
3/3 
final order within next 60 days, giving reasons in support
of the same.
With the afore-noted observation/ direction, the
writ petition stands dismissed.
