On 14.12.2022, we had passed the following order:
“Mrs. Binita Singh, learned counsel
for the BIADA states that the petitioner had only
purchased the plant and machinery and not the
land. Now, the land stands allotted to third party
and the unit is fully functional and operational.
However, the plant and machinery auction sold
in favour of the petitioner stands removed and
kept safely in the premises of BIADA. For some
reason, petitioner has not come forward to either
remove the machinery or take possession thereof
from BIADA. 
Despite  repeated  calls,  none  has
entered appearance on behalf of the petitioner. 
However, in the interest of justice,
matter is adjourned. 
List this case on 21 st of December,
2022, in the category of “Order Matters”.”
 
Even today, d espite repeated calls, none has entered
appearance on behalf of the petitioner. 
Petition is dismissed for default.
