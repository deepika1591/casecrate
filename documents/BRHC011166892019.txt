2 03-01-2023 Heard the learned counsel for the parties. 
The  licence  of  the  petitioner  was  cancelled,
against which order the petitioner preferred an appeal.
The Appellate Authority, for reasons recorded in
the order, remanded the case to the licensing authority to
pass a fresh order in accordance with law. Thereafter, the
licensing authority has again cancelled the licence of the
petitioner, against which an appeal lies.
The petitioner has approached this Court directly,
without preferring any appeal. 


Patna High Court CWJC No.23672 of 2019(2) dt.03-01-2023
2/2 
For this reason, we are not inclined to entertain
this petition.
If the petitioner so desires, he may file an appeal
against the order of cancellation of his licence within a
period of 60 days and in that event, the Appellate Authority
shall look into the matter and after providing sufficient
opportunity to the petitioner to present his cause, pass a
final order within next 60 days giving reasons in support of
the decision taken by him. 
The writ petition stands disposed of accordingly.
