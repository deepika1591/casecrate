ORAL ORDER
2 02-01-2023 1. The petitioner by way of this writ petition has
prayed to direct the Commission to issue Admit Card for
appearing in the selection process conducted by it in 2014 for
the post of Assistant Professor. It is stated that the petitioner has
passed Bihar Elementary Test for lecturership. On the basis
thereto, he was entitled to be considered. The ineligibility shown
for having not passed the B.E.T. is wrongful and unjustified.
2. Per contra, learned counsel submits that the writ
petition has been rendered infructous as the entire selection
process is over. Keeping in view the selection process has
already been concluded by the Commission and no selected
candidate has been impleaded as a party to the proceedings, it


Patna High Court CWJC No.14518 of 2015(2) dt.02-01-2023
2/2 
would be unfair for such selected candidates if this Court enters
into scrutinizing the selection process at this stage. However, the
question  raised  regarding  ineligibility  is  left  open  to  be
examined in future if need so arises.
3. The writ petition is dismissed as having become
infructuous.
