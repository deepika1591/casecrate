ORAL ORDER
(Per: HONOURABLE MR. JUSTICE ASHUTOSH KUMAR)
2 03-01-2023   Heard Mr. N.K. Agarwal, the learned senior
Advocate for the petitioner and Mr. Alok Ranjan, the learned
AC to AAG – 5 for the State. 
The petitioner was granted P.D.S. license after
his credentials were considered by the District Selection
Committee.  Later, on a complaint before the Lokayukta, the
matter was referred to the District Selection Committee


Patna High Court CWJC No.5181 of 2020(2) dt.03-01-2023
2/3
again for a fresh decision with respect to grant of license. 
The District Selection Committee recommended for
cancellation of license of the petitioner, pursuant to which
recommendation, the Licensing Authority has cancelled the
license of the petitioner. 
Several grounds have been raised by Mr. Agarwal,
the learned senior Advocate for the petitioner including the
fact that the jurisdiction exercised by the Lokayukta in
entertaining  any  complaint  against  the  petitioner  and
referring the matter to District Selection Committee for a
fresh consideration was without jurisdiction. 
Nonetheless, considering the fact that after fresh
consideration, respondent no. 7 has been granted license in
place of the petitioner, we deem it appropriate that the
matter be enquired into by the Divisional Commissioner.
This we say in view of the notification dated 21.07.2022
issued by the Government in exercise of the powers
conferred  under  Sections  3  and  5  of  the  Essential
Commodities Act, 1955 read with Clause -36 of the Bihar
Targeted Public Distribution System (Control) Order, 2016


Patna High Court CWJC No.5181 of 2020(2) dt.03-01-2023
3/3
and we direct that in the event of the petitioner making a
suitable representation / complaint before the concerned
Divisional Commissioner within a period of 30 days, he shall
look into the matter and after hearing all the stakeholders,
including respondent no. 7, shall pass a final order within a
further period of 60 days, giving reasons in support of the
decision taken by him. 
The order so passed by the authority shall be made
available to the petitioner as well as respondent no. 7
forthwith. 
We have specifically asked respondent no. 7 to be
heard by the Divisional Commissioner as the present order is
being passed in his absence. 
The writ petition stands disposed of accordingly.
