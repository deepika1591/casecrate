ORAL ORDER
5 02-01-2023 1. No one appears.
2. The petition has been preferred for quashing of the
order dated 14.12.2017, whereby the petitioner was asked to
give willingness within three days for transfer to another
School. Transfer being an incident of service, is not a subject
matter of judicial review, unless the same is effectuated by
malice or is barred by law.
3. Keeping in view above, this Court finds that the
petitioner has been asked to give her willingness to transfer after
long period as she was appointed in the year 1992.
4. Keeping in view the provisions, the order cannot be


Patna High Court CWJC No.1370 of 2018(5) dt.02-01-2023
2/2 
said to be illegal or unjustified.
5. The writ petition is mis-conceived and accordingly
dismissed.
