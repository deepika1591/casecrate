ORAL ORDER
4 04-01-2023 Heard the parties.
Petitioner has filed this writ petition for quashing the
order dated 11.04.2018 (Annexure-2) passed by the District
Magistrate,  Buxar,  the  Appellate  Authority,  in  Aanganwadi
Appeal case No. 72/ 2017 by which the Appellate Authority has
confirmed the order dated 20.04.2017 (Annexure-1) passed by
respondent No.2 and removed the petitioner from the post of
Aanganwari Sevika without any fault and on nonest ground.
Learned  counsel  for  the  State  submits  that  under
Section  12  of  Bihar  Aanganwadi  Sevika/  Sahaika  Chayan
Margdarshika, 2019 there is provision of revision before the
Divisional  Commissioner  against  the  order  passed  by  the
Appellate Authority and the petitioner has approached this court


Patna High Court CWJC No.16201 of 2018(4) dt.04-01-2023
2/2 
without availing such remedy.
In that view of the matter, I direct that the petitioner
may file revision before the Divisional Commissioner, Patna
against the order dated 11.04.2018 (Annexure-2) passed by  the
District Magistrate, Buxar and in case such revision is filed
within four weeks from today, the revision shall be disposed of
after hearing the parties by a reasoned and speaking order within
a period of six months thereafter without going into question of
delay, if any, which may have occurred in filing the revision.
This writ petition is disposed of in aforesaid terms.   
    
BKS/-
