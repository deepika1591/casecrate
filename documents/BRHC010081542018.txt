CORAM: HONOURABLE MR. JUSTICE ANIL KUMAR SINHA
ORAL ORDER
5 03-01-2023   Heard the parties. 
 Grievance of the petitioner is that Basgit Parcha
under Bihar Privileged Persons Homestead Tenancy Act has
wrongly been issued in favour of one Sakaldeep Paswan. 
Pursuant to the order dated 05.09.2022 passed by this
Court, the District Magistrate has constituted a committee
comprising of SDO, Mahnar and DCLR, Mahnar to conduct an
inquiry  and  pursuant  thereto  the  report  of  the  Enquiry
Committee was submitted on 21.10.2022 with the finding that
then Circle Officer, Desari was not correct in issuing the Basgit


Patna High Court CWJC No.5154 of 2018(5) dt.03-01-2023
2/2 
Parcha of the subject land in the name of Sakaldeep Paswan. 
It is an admitted position that the petitioner has filed
an appeal under Bihar Privileged Persons Homestead Tenancy
Act bearing Appeal Case No. 44/14-15 which is pending before
the Collector, Vaishali. 
In view of the statment made in the counter affidavit
by the Collector that the appeal will be decided expeditiously, I
direct the Collector, Vaishali to dispose the appeal on its merit
within  a  period  of  three  months  from  the  date  of
receipt/production of a copy of this order. 
With the above observation and direction, the petition
stands disposed of.
