2 02-01-2023   Petitioner has prayed for the following relief(s):
   “(i) For issuance of a writ in the nature
of certiorari for quashment of the judgment dated
04.02.2019 passed in OA 723 of 2017 by Debts
Recovery Tribunal (hereinafter called DRT), Patna
whereby  and  whereunder,  despite  non-joinder  of
insurance  Company,  a  joint  venture  of  the
respondent Bank and other irremediable infirmities


Patna High Court CWJC No.22929 of 2019(2) dt.02-01-2023
2/4 
in the documentation of loan, the Learned Presiding
Officer was pleased to allow the original application
filed u/s 19 of Recovery of Debts and Bankruptcy
Act, 1993 (hereinafter called DRT Act) or in the
alternative,  it  is  prayed  that  the  matter  may  be
remanded back to the DRT, Patna for fresh haring
after  impleadment  of  insurance  Company  in  the
array of defendants.
(ii) For holding that the account of the
petitioner Company was Standard according to RBI
guidelines and IRAC Norms before unprecedented
floods in river Ganga and its tributaries in the year
2016 kept the factory and its godown sub- merged
for more than a month causing irreparable extensive
damage to not only plant and machinery but also to
the finished items, other raw materials and civil
construction resulting into a cumulative loss of more
than Rs. 60 crore.
(iii)  For  holding  that  the  insurance
Company  namely  Universal  Sompo  General
Insurance Company Limited, a joint venture of the
respondent  Bank,  being  a  necessary  party  was
intentionally not arrayed as a defendant in OA No.
723/2017 despite written as well as oral submission
to  this  effect  at  the  bar  in  DRT,  Patna,  the
appearance of which was critical to the judicious
adjudication of the original application.
(iv) For holding that non settlement of the
claim for more than three years has come as  a


Patna High Court CWJC No.22929 of 2019(2) dt.02-01-2023
3/4 
double whammy for the petitioner Company in that
on one hand the force majeure in the form of floods
damaged everything in the Company and on the
other  hand,  the  respondent  Bank  finding  the
petitioner in vulnerable position not only enforced
provisions  under  SARFAESI  Act  but  also
disingenuously retrieved judgment in OA No. 723
of 2017 by rigging the law.
(v)  For  any  other  relief  or  reliefs  for
which the petitioner may be deemed entitled to in
the eyes of law.
And, in the interim
(vi) The petitioner prays for stay of the
operation  of  the  impugned  judgment  dated
04.02.2019 in OA No. 723 of 2017, which has been
rechristened as RP No. 167 of 2019 pending before
Recovery Officer, DRT, Patna.”
                In view of the fact that now D.R.A.T. is functional,
learned counsel for the petitioner, under instructions, states that,
the petition may be permitted to be withdrawn, with liberty to
file an appeal before the D.R.A.T. or to initiate appropriate
proceedings before the appropriate forum and to take recourse
to such other alternate remedies which are equally efficacious in
law.
Prayer allowed.
Petition is disposed of as withdrawn with the liberty
