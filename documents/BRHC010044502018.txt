3 03-01-2023 1. No one appears on behalf of the petitioner.
2. The petitioner by way of this writ petition prays for
quashing of the Guest Teacher Merit List of Zoology, dated
13.10.2018. The petitioner admittedly submitted his Form which
was not accepted. Since the petitioner has not participated in the
selection process. No case for interference with the merit list
can be said to be made out, which has already become operative
since 2018. 
3. The writ petition is accordingly dismissed.
