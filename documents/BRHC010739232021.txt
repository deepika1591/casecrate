CORAM: HONOURABLE MR. JUSTICE PURNENDU SINGH
ORAL ORDER
3 03-01-2023 Heard Mr. Pramod Kumar Thakur, learned counsel
appearing on behalf of the petitioner and Mr. Sajid Salim Khan,
learned SC-25 for the respondent.
The petitioner has prayed for following relief in the
present writ petition:-
“That, this application is being filed
for kind indulgence of this Hon’ble Court by way
of issuance of an appropriate Writ/s, Order/s,
Direction/s upon the Respondents for quashing of
Notice dated 15.09.2021, bearing Gyapank No.
1915 dated 16.09.2021, issued by the Circle
Officer, Naugachia (Respondent No.6), by which he
has been pleased to direct the petitioner to vacate


Patna High Court CWJC No.18412 of 2021(3) dt.03-01-2023
2/6 
the encroachment of Road within ten (10) days,
otherwise it would be vacated by the Respondents
themselves at the cost of petitioner.
AND
For any other relief/reliefs, for which the petitioner
may be found fit and proper either in the eye of law
or among the facts.”
Learned  counsel  appearing  on  behalf  of  the
petitioner submitted that a false complaint was made with
respect to encroachment before the Sub Divisional Public
Complain Grievance Office, Naugachia. The Public Grievance
Officer directed the Circle Officer, Naugachia and the Anchal
Amin to make a spot enquiry relating to old plot no.
508,509,510 and 511 which has been shown by the Circle
Officer as new plot no. 812 appertaining to khata no. 943 and
942. The petitioner was served with notice dated 15.09.2021.
Learned  counsel  appearing  on  behalf  of  the
petitioner submitted that the petitioner has ancestral house on
the old plot no. 508,509,510 and 511 appertaining to old khata
no. 230 measuring 41 decimal and has been enjoying the said
property for more than 100 years. The house constructed by his
grandfather has been allotted in favour of the father of the
petitioner after partition of family by metes and bounds. The old
kheshra’s mentioned above are also recorded in the name of the
great-grandfather of the petitioner late Gurucharan Kapri in


Patna High Court CWJC No.18412 of 2021(3) dt.03-01-2023
3/6 
cadastral survey khatiyan and due to certain incorrect entries
made in the revisional survey khatiyan 14 decimal of the land
has been recorded as “Gair Majarua Aam Sarva Sadharan”. 
Learned  counsel  appearing  on  behalf  of  the
petitioner further submitted that the petitioner has filed Title
Suit No. 137 of 2019 against the State of Bihar, claiming that
revisional survey plot no. 812 appertaining to khata no. 942 is
his khatiyani land. The Government has deliberately not filed
their written statement in the Title Suit, but at the same time
they have illegally proceeded with anti encroachment drive in
which certain portion of the ancestral house of the petitioner has
been demolished. 
It is further submitted by the learned counsel
appearing on behalf of the petitioner that no show-cause notice
was served to the petitioner in accordance with the provisions of
the Bihar Public Land Encroachment Act, 1956, and in violation
of principle of natural justice.
Mr. Sajid Salim Khan, learned SC-25, appearing on
behalf of the State submitted that he has not received any
instruction as to whether pursuant to the notice under Section
80, C.P.C, the State has filed their written statement in the Title
Suit No. 137 of 2019. He further submitted that from the records


Patna High Court CWJC No.18412 of 2021(3) dt.03-01-2023
4/6 
it appears that a public petition was received in Anchal office for
removal of encroachment. The Circle Officer has proceeded
after conducting spot verification and after measuring the area,
encroachments  were  removed  from  the  public  land  on
03.03.2022 in accordance with law.
He further submitted that records reveals that an
injunction petition under order-XXXIX, Rules 1&2 read with
Section 151 of Code of Civil Procedure filed by the petitioner
before the learned Sub-Judge-I, Naugachia in the Title Suit No.
137 of 2019 was rejected by the trial court. He further submitted
that if the petitioner has any grievance against the notices or the
action taken by the Circle Officer with respect to Bihar Public
Land Encroachment Act, 1956, he has an efficacious remedy
under Section 11 of the Bihar Public Land Encroachment Act,
1956 and submitted that the present writ petition is not
maintainable in the eye of law.
Heard the parties
The record reveals that the petitioner has resorted
to file Title Suit No. 137 of 2019 in which the State Government
has not filed their written statement, further the injunction under
order-XXXIX, Rules 1&2 read with Section 151 of Code of
Civil Procedure was not granted to the petitioner. The ancestral


Patna High Court CWJC No.18412 of 2021(3) dt.03-01-2023
5/6 
house of the petitioner stands on new plot no. 812 appertaining
to khata no. 942 and a portion of the ancestral house of the
petitioner has been demolished.
This Court finds that primarily the matter relates to
incorrect entry made in the revisional survey khatiyan which has
been objected by the petitioner, who has challenged the same by
filing Title Suit, which is pending in court of learned Sub-Judge-
I, Naugachia bearing Title Suit No. 137 of 2019. It was not
proper  on the part of the State to initiate a proceeding under
Bihar Public Land Encroachment Act, 1956 in which certain
orders have been passed and the certain portion of the ancestral
house of the petitioner has been demolished without conducting
any measurement.
This Court finds that the District Magistrate,
Bhagalpur without rectifying the land records and also ignorant
of the fact that Title Suit is pending in which the State has not
filed written statement till date as per the averment made,  has
illegally proceeded to demolish the certain portion of the
ancestral house constructed over the land belonging to the share
of the petitioner which is subject matter of the suit. Therefore, in
such circumstances, it would be proper to direct the District
Magistrate, Bhagalpur to ventilate their stand by filing written


Patna High Court CWJC No.18412 of 2021(3) dt.03-01-2023
6/6 
statement in the suit, so as, not to further linger the Title Suit
proceeding in the court of 1 st Additional Judge, Bhagalpur and
restrain from arbitrary action. 
The present writ petition is disposed of with a
direction to the respondent State authorities not to disturb the
peaceful possession of the petitioner over the land in question
nor shall demolish any construction there upon until Title Suit
No. 137 of 2019 is decided, which is pending in the court of
learned Sub-Judge-I, Bhagalpur.  Petitioner, if so advised, may
file  amendment  of  relief  for  seeking  compensation  and
restoration of the possession. The trial court may pass necessary
order in accordance with law to avoid multiplicity of the suits in
view of the law laid down by the Apex Court in the case of B.K.
Narayana  Pillai  vs  Pararneswaran  Pillai  &Anr  reported in
(2000) 1 SCC 712 . The Title Suit shall be decided by the trial
court on its own merits in accordance with the pleading and
evidences of the parties without being influenced by this order.
