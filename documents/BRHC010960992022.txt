3 02-01-2023   Shri  Anjani  Kumar,  learned  AAG-4,  inviting
attention of Bihar Contractors Registration Rules, 2007, states
that,  should  the  petitioner  take  recourse  to  the  remedies  of
preferring an appeal before the Statutory Authority within next
one week, the appellate authority shall positively consider and
decide the same, with the petitioner fully cooperating, within
two weeks thereafter.
As mutually  agreed,  petition  is  disposed  of  in  the

(a) Petitioner shall approach the authority concerned

to the petitioner;

(f) Equally, liberty is reserved to the petitioner to take
recourse  to  such  alternative  remedies  as  are
otherwise available in accordance with law; 
(g) We are hopeful that as and when petitioner takes
recourse  to  such  remedies,  as  are  otherwise
