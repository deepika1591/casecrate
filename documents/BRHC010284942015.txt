CORAM: HONOURABLE MR. JUSTICE PRABHAT KUMAR SINGH
ORAL ORDER
3 02-01-2023   Heard learned counsel for the petitioners and the
State.   
Instant  writ petition has been filed seeking direction


Patna High Court CWJC No.13629 of 2015(3) dt.02-01-2023
2/3 
upon the respondents for payment of arrears of honorarium to
the  petitioners from the date of their appointment, i.e.,
22.10.2014 till date with all consequential benefits.
It is submitted on behalf of the  petitioners that the
petitioners belong to Mahadalit and Minority Community. In
pursuance to  Letter No. 598 dated 23.10.2014 (A nnexure 9)
issued by the Block Education Officer, Turkaulia (respondent
no.6)  petitioners were selected and appointed the  post of Tola
Shevak and Siksha Swaymshevak respectively in the District of
Motihari, East Champaran. Said letter was also sent the District
Programme  Officer  (Literacy)  Motihari,  East  Champaran.
Learned counsel for the  petitioners further submits that the
petitioners were appointed after following all procedure and
they discharged their duties to the satisfaction of the concerned
authorities without any adverse remarks, still they have not been
paid honorarium since 22.10.2014.
Counter  affidavit  has  been  filed  on  behalf  of
respondents no. 4 to 6. It has been stated that in fact Letter No.
598 dated 23.10.2014 (A nnexure 9) by which  petitioners had
been selected was not approved by the District Programme
Officer and in spite of non-approval, the School Selection
Committee of Tola sevak Sanchalan Sammittee issued Niyojan


Patna High Court CWJC No.13629 of 2015(3) dt.02-01-2023
3/3 
letter to the  petitioners, as such, Niyojan of 19 Tola Sevaks and
Educational Volunteers including the  petitioners, has been
cancelled. Furthermore, order of the District Programme Officer
was never challenged before the writ court. 
Since  petitioners’s selection and appointment were
never given mandatory approval by the competent authority,
writ petition, being devoid of merit, is accordingly, dismissed.  
  
    
Shashi
