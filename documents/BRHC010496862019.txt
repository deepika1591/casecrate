ORAL ORDER
(Per: HONOURABLE MR. JUSTICE ASHUTOSH KUMAR)
2 03-01-2023 Heard the learned counsel for the parties.
Pursuant to the petitioner having been made accused
in a criminal case, his licence was suspended under Clause 28 of
the Bihar Targetted Public Distribution System (Control) Order,
2016. Ever since then, no notice has been served upon the
petitioner. 
However, Mr. N. K. Agarwal, the learned Senior
Advocate for the petitioner fairly concedes that this writ
application was filed in 2019 and he is not aware of the latest


Patna High Court CWJC No.11605 of 2019(2) dt.03-01-2023
2/2 
position, namely, whether any final order has been passed. In
any event, the petitioner has not been served with any notice.
Under the aforesaid circumstances, we direct the
licensing authority to serve notice on the petitioner within a
period of 30 days from the date of receipt/production of a copy
of this order, which shall be accepted by the petitioner without
any demur and he shall reply to the same within a further period
of 15 days.
On receipt of such reply by the petitioner, the
licensing authority shall pass a final order within next 60 days
but not without giving reasons in support of his conclusions.
It is also made clear, however, that in case any final
order has already been passed, this order shall not be given
effect to and in that event if the petitioner remains dissatisfied,
he could avail of his remedies available under the Bihar
Targeted Public Distribution System (Control) Order, 2016.
The petition stands disposed of accordingly.
