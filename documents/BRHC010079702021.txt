ORAL ORDER
2 02-01-2023 Petitioner claims to be the owner of the land bearing
Khata No. 1, Survey Plot No. 1246, 1255 and Khata No. 61,
Survey Plot No. 1272 & 1273.
Contention of the petitioner is that  on 10.06.2020 he
filed  a  petition  before  the  Circle  Officer,  Hayaghat  for
demarcation of his land described therein but no action has been
taken by the Circle Officer to demarcate the petitioner’s land to
enable him to construct a boundary.
Learned counsel for the State submits that the Circle


Patna High Court CWJC No.6827 of 2021(2) dt.02-01-2023
2/2 
Officer, Hayaghat, District- Darbhanga  be directed to consider
the claim of the petitioner for demarcation in accordance with
law.
Taking into consideration the nature of relief claimed
by the petitioner and the fact that petition for demarcation of the
land is still pending before the Circle Officer since 10.06.2020,
the Circle Officer, Hayaghat, District - Darbhanga / respondent
no. 6  is directed to take necessary steps in accordance with law
for demarcation of the land in question after giving opportunity
to all concerned preferably within a period of three months from
the date of receipt / production of a copy of this order.
With the aforesaid observation and direction, this writ
petition is disposed of.
