ORAL ORDER
4 02-01-2023 1.   No one appears for the petitioner. 
2. The writ petition has been preferred, praying for
taking necessary steps alleging that respondent no. 4 had
appeared in the Matriculation and Intermediate Examination
several times by changing name. 
3. The  jurisdiction of Court  under Article 226 cannot
be involved for personal vengeance . The forum used is gross
abuse of legal process. The petitioner is seeking to make a
complaint against respondent no. 4 and initiating the Court
process, so that he may be able to take advantage in relation to
property dispute which he admits to be pending as per Para 4 of


Patna High Court CWJC No.2955 of 2018(4) dt.02-01-2023
2/2 
the writ petition. 
4. The writ petition is accordingly dismissed with cost
of Rs. 20,000 (Twenty thousand), to be recovered from the
petitioner by way of P.D.R. proceedings.
