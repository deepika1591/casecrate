ORAL ORDER
2 02-01-2023 1. The petitioner has assailed the order that the
respondent  was  appointed  in  place  of  the  petitioner  as
Anganwadi worker. It is informed that while after filing of this
writ petition, the petitioner has also filed an appeal before the
concerned Collector who is authorized to decide such disputes.
2. Learned counsel submits that direction be issued to
the District Magistrate Siwan to decide the appeal.
3. Learned counsel appearing for the State submits


Patna High Court CWJC No.14491 of 2015(2) dt.02-01-2023
2/2 
that he would be satisfied if a direction is issued in this regard,
otherwise the writ petition is not maintainable.
4. Keeping in view that the post of Anganwadi worker
is not a cadre post, the writ petition would not lie for the said
purpose. However, as an appeal has already been preferred
before the concerned District Magistrate, it is directed that he
shall decide the dispute raised by the petitioner after giving
opportunity to the other side within a period of two months
henceforth.
5. The writ petition is disposed of accordingly.
