CORAM: HONOURABLE MR. JUSTICE PRABHAT KUMAR SINGH
ORAL ORDER
3 02-01-2023   Heard the parties.
Petitioner has filed this writ petition for quashing the
order dated 01.08.2011 (Annexure-2) passed by respondent
No.5, by which appointment of the petitioner as Kachhari
Secretary has been cancelled.
Learned counsel for the State submits that under
Section 12 & 13 of Bihar  Gram Kachhari Sachiv (Employment,
Service Conditions & Duty) Rules, 2007 there is provision of
complaint before the  concerned Sub divisional Magistrate and
appeal before the District Magistrate and the petitioner has


Patna High Court CWJC No.19412 of 2018(3) dt.02-01-2023
2/2 
approached this court without availing such remedy.
In that view of the matter, I direct that the petitioner
may file appeal before the District Magistrate, Samastipur
against the order dated 01.08.2011 (Annexure-2) passed by
respondent No.5, the Block Development Officer, Kalyanpur
and in case such appeal is filed within four weeks from today,
the appeal shall be disposed of after hearing the parties by a
reasoned and speaking order within a period of six months
thereafter without going into question of delay, if any, which
may have occurred in filing the appeal.
This writ petition is disposed of in aforesaid terms. 
    
BKS/-
