Petitioner has prayed for the following relief(s):
“I. That, for setting aside the office
order contained in Memo No. 1033/Jamui, dt.
19.6.18,  communicated  to  the  petitioner  after
four  months,  through  registered  post  on
11.10.18,  whereby  and  where  under  security
deposit  to  the  tune  of  Rs.1062379/-  of  the
petitioner  has  been  forfeited  against  his  final
bill.
II.  That,  payment  further  prays  for
direction upon the respondents for payment of

final bill including forfeited security deposit and
apart  from  that  final  payment  should  be
calculated after adding 18% interest per annum
for the delayed period right from the date when
it was due to the date of its final realization.
III. That, respondents may kindly be
punished for their deliberate act of harassing this
petitioner. Petitioner has already completed the
entire  work  as  per  specification  agreed  in
between  the  parties,  in  presence  of
officials/respondents  of  Rural  Works
Department in 2018, i.e. much after the so called
order of termination of contract. At one hand
they have terminated the agreement, and on the
other hand they have remained been taking work
from the petitioner even after so called order of
termination of contract.
IV.  That,  for  any  other  relief  for
which petitioner is found entitled in the facts
and circumstances of this case.”
In view of the fact that Bihar Public Works Dispute
Arbitration Tribunal is now functional, learned counsel for the
petitioner, under instructions, seeks permission to withdraw the
present  petition  with  liberty  to  take  recourse  to  such  other
appropriate remedies, as are otherwise available, in accordance
with law. 
Permission granted. 
As  and  when  any  such  request  is  made  by  the

petitioner before the Tribunal, the same shall be considered and
decided expeditiously.
Mrs. Archana Meenakshee, learned GP 6, states that if
the application is filed within a period of four weeks from today,
limitation  shall  not  be  allowed  to  come  in  the  way  of  its
consideration and decision on merits since the petitioner has
been pursuing remedies before this Hon’ble Court.  
Statement accepted and taken on record. 
The petition stands disposed of as withdrawn with the
liberty aforesaid.
Interlocutory application(s), if any, shall also stand
disposed of.
