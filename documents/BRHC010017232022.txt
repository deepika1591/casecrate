4 03-01-2023   Petitioner has prayed for the following relief (s) :- 
“1. That the petitioner beseeches indulgence of
this Hon'ble Court with a prayer for issuance of
appropriate writ or writs in the nature of certiorari
for setting aside the office order no. 119 as contained
in Memo No. 1460, dt. 17.12.2021 issued by the
Chief Engineer (Project - 1), Bihar State Power
Transmission Company Ltd., Patna, whereby the
petitioner in JV with M/S REW Contracts Pvt. Ltd.
has been ordered to be debarred from participating in


Patna High Court CWJC No.890 of 2022(4) dt.03-01-2023
2/3 
future tender of BSPTCL till satisfactory completion
of all works against NIT No. 46/PR/BSPTCL/2018,
which  order  simultaneously  communicates  of
termination of contract in case there was no
assurance for supply of balance 05 numbers of 200
MVA Transformer within a week from the date of the
order, which is quite arbitrary, callous, unreasonable
and  without  consideration  of  the  facts  and
circumstances of the case, and using the predominant
position of being a government company to coerce
the employee/contractor to abide by own terms,
ignoring  ground  realities  and  the  intervening
circumstances in price rise due to COVID -19 and
scuttling the whole process to get rid of and to
oppress the petitioner with heavy civil consequence
due to the impugned order, which is in most arbitrary
and unreasonable manner, without giving any
personal hearing in compliance of principles of
natural justice and as a matter of fact, the said
debarment order was passed only to deny the
escalation price of 200 MVA transformers to
petitioner JV as was being requested time and again
and the impugned order of debarment is for an
indefinite period, i.e., till satisfactory completion of
work and this order has been issued unilaterally
assigning lapses in execution of contract for which
Respondent Corporation might have other remedies,
yet, it resorted to this coercive and oppressive
method becoming judge of its own cause and acted
unilaterally, ignoring the concerns/issues raised by
petitioner JV as well as passing the order without
assigning reasons to deny the issues, and at the same
time fastening the debarment order with termination
notice, which is completely illegal, invalid, arbitrary
and disproportionate violating Articles 14, 19 (1) (g)
as well as 21 of the Constitution of India.”
Learned counsel for the respondents states that
impugned order dated 17.12.2021 (Annexure14 Page-69) stands
recalled vide  office order No. 105 dated 30.12.2022, as such,
