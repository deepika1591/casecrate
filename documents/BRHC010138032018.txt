CORAM: HONOURABLE MR. JUSTICE PRABHAT KUMAR SINGH
ORAL ORDER
4. 02-01-2023  This writ application has been filed against the order
dated  10.07.2018,  communicated  through  HO/DAD/11/18-
19/No.136 by the General Manager, Uttar Bihar Gramin Bank,
Muzaffarpur (hereinafter referred to as “Bank”), whereby and
whereunder the appeal preferred by the petitioner has been
rejected, as the same was filed after a lapse of stipulated time of
45 days, which is not considerable in terms of regulation 49 of
Uttar Bihar Gramin Bank (Officers & Employees) Service
Regulation,  2010  r/w  Uttar  Bihar  Gramin  Bank  Service
(Amendment)  Regulation,  2013  (Annexure  8  to  the  writ
petition).
Learned counsel for the petitioner submits that the


Patna High Court CWJC No.19432 of 2018(4) dt.02-01-2023
2/3 
appeal, preferred by the petitioner, has not been considered on
merit, rather the same has been dismissed on technical ground.
He further submits that due to ailments and surgery, the
petitioner could not come to know about the dismissal order of
the disciplinary authority and as such, could not file the appeal
on time.
Learned counsel for the respondent/Bank vehemently
opposes the prayer made in the writ petition and submits that the
appeal has been rightly dismissed by the authority concerned
in terms of regulation 49 of Uttar Bihar Gramin Bank (Officers
& Employees) Service Regulation, 2010, as the appeal was
preferred after a lapse of more than three years.
Considering the submissions made on behalf of the
parties and the materials available on record, it is an admitted
position that the appeal, preferred by the petitioner, has not been
decided on merit, rather the same stood dismissed on the ground
of limitation. 
In view of reasons assigned by the petitioner for filing
delayed appeal, the writ petition is allowed and impugned order
dated 10.07.2018 (Annexure 8) is quashed with a direction to
the Board of Directors of the Bank / competent authority to
consider the appeal of the petitioner on merit. If the petitioner


Patna High Court CWJC No.19432 of 2018(4) dt.02-01-2023
3/3 
files an appeal before the competent authority within a period of
four weeks from today, the competent authority, without being
prejudiced, shall consider the same and pass appropriate order in
accordance with law.
