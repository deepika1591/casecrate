ORAL ORDER
(Per: HONOURABLE MR. JUSTICE ASHUTOSH KUMAR)
2 02-01-2023 Heard the learned counsel for the petitioner and
the State.
The license of the petitioner was cancelled on
various charges for which show-cause notice was issued to
him but he did not respond. The order passed by the
licensing authority has been upheld in appeal as well as in
the revision. 
The only contention of the petitioner is that the
first order was passed without any show-cause reply. 


Patna High Court CWJC No.14535 of 2017(2) dt.02-01-2023
2/2 
There is no averment in the writ petition that
notice was not issued to the petitioner. 
That the petitioner was not served notice could be
a ground but not in the present set of circumstances where
there is an allegation that the petitioner does not even reside
at the place where the shop is situated and the explanation
offered by the petitioner that at the relevant time he was
facing criminal prosecution and therefore had gone fugitive
is not acceptable. 
However, on hearing the observations of the
Bench, learned Advocate for the petitioner seeks permission
to withdraw this application in order to agitate his claim
before the Secretary, Food & Consumer Protection
Department, Bihar, Patna, if so desired.
The application stands dismissed as withdrawn
with the aforesaid liberty.
