Heard learned counsel for the parties.
Petitioner has prayed for the following relief(s):- 
“1. That the instant writ petition has been
brought by the petitioner for issuance of appropriate
writ/direction to the respondents directing them to pay
Rs. 51,95,785/- of current A/C of late Krishna Prasad


Patna High Court CWJC No.22596 of 2019 dt.02-01-2023
2/3 
with interest to his wife namely Malti Gupta which is
retained knowingly, deliberately and illegally since
2017 at State Bank of India Exhibition Road Branch,
Patna-800001.”
In view of the response filed by the respondent
Bank, and more so averments made in paragraph nos. 8 and
9 that the petitioner has not completed the formalities as are
statutorily required to be complied with for substitution of
the name as also release of the amount in favour of the legal
heirs of the deceased account holder, learned counsel for the
petitioner states that the petitioner shall make himself
available in the office of respondent no.6, namely, Branch
Manager, State Bank of India, Exhibition Road Branch,
Patna-800001.
Learned counsel for the respondent Bank states
that with the petitioner completing all the formalities, of
which he shall be appraised of, decision shall positively be
taken on the petitioner’s request within a period of one
week and amount found due and payable along with
interest,  if  any,  shall  be  remitted  directly  into  the
petitioner’s bank account.


Patna High Court CWJC No.22596 of 2019 dt.02-01-2023
3/3 
As such, the instant writ petition stands disposed
of in the aforesaid terms.
Interlocutory Application(s), if any, shall stand
disposed of.
