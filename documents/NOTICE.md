# Source and licence

These 300 judgments come from the **Indian High Court Judgments** open
dataset on AWS Open Data, licensed **CC-BY-4.0**.

- Dataset: https://registry.opendata.aws/indian-high-court-judgments/
- Court: Patna High Court, year 2023
- Each judgment's own source URL is in `documents.json`.

Indian court judgments are additionally outside copyright under section 52(1)(q)
of the Copyright Act, 1957. Text was extracted from the original PDFs; no
