ORAL ORDER
12 03-01-2023 Learned counsel for the petitioner and the State are
present.   
At the outset, learned counsel for the  petitioner prays
for disposal of the writ petition with liberty to the  petitioner to
file a representation with respect to his grievances contained in
the  writ petition, before  the Principal Secretary, Building
Construction Department, Government of Bihar, Bailey Road,
Patna (respondent no.1). 
Let  the  petitioner  file  such  representation  before
respondent no.1 within a period of four weeks from today. 
In the event aforesaid representation is filed by the
petitioner, respondent no.1 shall look into the matter and dispose
of the same by a reasoned and speaking order within a period of
further six months. 
 Writ petition stands disposed of with the aforesaid
observation.
