ORAL ORDER
3 03-01-2023   Heard the parties.
1. The petitioners have preferred this writ petition
seeking quashing of the order dated 20.09.2010 passed by the
One Man Enquiry Committee, whereby the One Man Enquiry
Committee  refused  to  entertain  their  application  for
intervention.
2. Learned counsel submits that the petitioners are at
the same footing as those whose cases were to be examined by
the One Man Enquiry Committee and he was obliged to accept
the intervention application and also examine the cases of the
petitioners with regard to continuation and reinstatement in
service.
3. I have considered the submission. It appears that


Patna High Court CWJC No.23823 of 2012(3) dt.03-01-2023
4/6 
the petitioners were appointed on Class-III and Class-IV post in
a  Tuberculosis  Centre  without  following  any  procedure.
Thereafter, they continued up to the year 1993. On 30 th April
1993, the Department of Health issued an order holding that the
appointments which were made by Dr. A. A. Mallick without
following due procedure, were liable to be cancelled and
consequential termination orders were issued. Several other
persons challenged their termination order which writ petitions
were dismissed by this Court in a judgment reported in 1995 (2)
P.L.J.R. 309 in Lalan Kumar Singh & Ors Vrs. State of
Bihar & Ors . S.L.P. was preferred which was disposed of by
the Supreme Court on 16.12.1996 and the issue stood closed
reported in 1997 (2) S.C.C. Page 1 Ashwani Kumar & Ors.
Vrs. State of Bihar & Ors.
4. So far as petitioners are concerned, admittedly they
did not challenge their termination either before this Court or
before the Supreme Court. However, those who had challenged
in view of  observations  made  by  the  Apex  Court were
considered by the Government for reinstatement and after a
passage of time their cases were also re-examined and their
services were dispensed with in 2003. The writ petitions were
filed which came to be allowed against which L.P.A. was filed


Patna High Court CWJC No.23823 of 2012(3) dt.03-01-2023
5/6 
before the Division Bench and the Division Bench constituted
One Man Enquiry Committee headed by retired High Court
Judge who was asked to examine each and every individual
matter and method and manner in which the person was
appointed or reinstated. While examining those cases, the
petitioners moved an I.A. seeking intervention and claiming the
same benefit as being given to the others who had been
reinstated or re-appointed by the State Government. It is also
noticed that the petitioners moved an intervention application at
this stage of L.P.A. also. However, the One Man Enquiry
Committee has refused to accept the application and entertain
their claim. The present writ petition has been filed claiming the
same party as given to others.
5. This Court finds that the claim of the petitioner is
not made out. They have never challenged their removal from
service made in 1993 in any Court. Removal of persons in the
year 1993 came to be affirmed by the Division Bench of this
Court as reported in 1995 (2) P.L.J.R. 309  in Lalan Kumar
Singh & Ors Vrs. State of Bihar & Ors.  which was also
affirmed by the Supreme Court in Kumar Vs. State of Bihar and
those  cases  of  the  petitioner  would  be  similar  to  those
challenged in 1993 termination. Keeping in view thereto, the


Patna High Court CWJC No.23823 of 2012(3) dt.03-01-2023
6/6 
view taken by the One Man Enquiry Committee not to entertain
the intervention petition and examine cases of petitioners cannot
be faulted. They are distinct class and cannot claim parity to
those who were reinstated or freshly appointed or absorbed
subsequently. In view thereof, the writ petition filed by the
petitioners seeking reinstatement by way of intervention after
having been terminated from service in the year 1993 by filing a
writ petition in 2012 is wholly mis-concieved and is accordingly
dismissed.
