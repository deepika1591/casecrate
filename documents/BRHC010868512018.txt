CORAM: HONOURABLE MR. JUSTICE PRABHAT KUMAR SINGH
ORAL ORDER
3 03-01-2023  Petitioner seeks quashing of order dated 17.5.2018,
passed by the District Programme Officer, Gaya ( respondent
no.4) in Anganbari Case No. 79 of 2015, contained in Annexure
4, by which  petitioner’s engagement as Anganbari Sevika has
been terminated.
At the outset, referring to paragraph 5 of the counter
affidavit, learned counsel appearing for the  State  submits that
the petitioner, without availing the remedy of appeal before
District Magistrate, Gaya (respondent no.3) under Rule 12 of
the Aanganwari Sevika/Sahaika Chayan Margdarsika, 2019, has
straightway filed the writ petition.  
 In view of the aforesaid submission,  writ petition is
disposed of with direction to the  petitioner to file an appeal
before  respondent no.3 against the impugned order dated
17.5.2018 within a period of four weeks from today. If such
