2 04-01-2023 Petitioner has prayed for the following relief/s :-
“(i) quashing the order(s) dated 12.09.2019
and ), passed by the respondent no. 3 & 4, namely,
Head  of  Territory  Office,  Bharat  Petroleum
Corporation Limited, by which the application of the

been rejected, on a premise that the site offered by the

2/3 
is beyond 5 kms from Mabbi Chowk towards
Kamtaul on SH 75, District-Darbhanga;
(ii) to direct the respondent to reconsider
the application of the petitioner for award of Retail
Outlet dealership, in view of the fact that the land
offered by the petitioner is within 5 kms from Mabbi
Chowk towards Kamtaul on SH 75, District –

lottery among eligible candidates;
(iii) pass such other order/orders as your
Lordship may deem fit and proper in the facts and
circumstances of the present case.”
After the matter was heard for some time, learned
counsel for the petitioner seeks permission to withdraw the
present petition reserving liberty to take recourse to such other
remedies as are otherwise permissible and admissible in
accordance with law as also represent respondent No.4, namely,
the Territory Manager (Retail) Bharat Petroleum Corporation
Limited, Muzaffarpur (Retail) Territory, Village Sherpur, Near
Narayanpur Anant Railway Station, on NH 28, P.O. MIC Bela,
Muzaffarpur, venting out the grievances, subject-matter of the
present petition.
Prayer allowed. Liberty, as prayed for, is granted.
Without expressing any opinion on merits, leaving all
questions of fact and law open, the present petition is disposed

3/3 
of as withdrawn with the liberty aforesaid.
We expect that the appropriate authority shall consider
and decide the petitioner’s application/request expeditiously and
preferably within a period of three months from the date of its
presentation.
Needless to say that while considering such request,
principles of natural justice shall be followed and due
opportunity of hearing afforded to the parties. 
If aggrieved by the said order, the petitioner shall
have liberty to approach this Court by way of separate
petition(s), if so required and desired.
Interlocutory application, if any, shall also stand
disposed of.
