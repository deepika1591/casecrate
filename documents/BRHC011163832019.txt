ORAL ORDER
(Per: HONOURABLE MR. JUSTICE ASHUTOSH KUMAR)
3 03-01-2023 Heard the learned counsel for the parties. 
Mr. Madhav Jha, the learned Advocate for the
petitioner has pointed out that notwithstanding the fact that
the caste certificate offered by respondent no.8 was faulty,
she was chosen to be granted licence and the petitioner was
left high and dry.
In view of the nature of the controversy raised
with respect to grant of licence to respondent no.8 and in


Patna High Court CWJC No.24595 of 2019(3) dt.03-01-2023
2/2 
view of the notification dated 21.07.2022 issued by the
Government in exercise of the powers conferred under
Sections 3 and 5 of the Essential Commodities Act, 1955
read  with  Clause-36  of  the  Bihar  Targeted  Public
Distribution System (Control) Order, 2016, we direct that in
the  event  of  the  petitioner  making  a  suitable
representation/complaint before the concerned Divisional
Commissioner within a period of 30 days, the Divisional
Commissioner shall look into the matter and after hearing
all the stakeholders, including respondent No.8, shall pass a
final order within next 60 days, giving reasons in support of
the decision taken by him.
We have specifically asked for respondent no.8 to
be heard by the Divisional Commissioner for the reason that
this order has been passed in her absence.
With the afore-noted direction, the writ petition
stands disposed off.
