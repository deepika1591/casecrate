CORAM: HONOURABLE MR. JUSTICE DR. ANSHUMAN
ORAL ORDER
3 02-01-2023 Heard learned counsel for the petitioner and learned
counsel for the State.  
Learned counsel for the petitioner has filed the present
writ petition with the following reliefs:
“(i). For a direction to the respondents authorities to
pay the difference amount of 3 % of the dearness
allowance as applicable on the date of retirement on
the amount paid against the head of gratuity and
earned leave.
(ii). Also for commanding the respondent to make
payment of the arrears of the salary for the period

Patna High Court CWJC No.13205 of 2021(3) dt.02-01-2023
2/3 
April, 2007 to June 2008, which has not been paid
till date.
(iii). Also for grant of monetary benefit accrue to the
petitioner a far grant of ACP by fixing the pay on
promotional pay scale with all consequential benefits
arising thereon.
(iv). Also to commanding the respondent authorities
to payment of interest over the aforesaid due amount
@ 12 % per annum.
(v). For any other relief/ reliefs if petitioner found
entitle in the facts and circumstances of the present
case.”
From the record, it transpires that the petitioner has
retired on 31.01.2019 from the post of Lecturer from District
Panchayati Raj Training Centre, Gaya. It is no where mentioned
in the petition that on what post he was initially joined. His date
of joining has not been mentioned in the petition. But the
petitioner has demanded the arrear of salary as well as the claim
for grant of ACP with effect from 03.11.2017.
Learned counsel for the State submits that during the
pendency of the writ petition, all admitted dues of the petitioner
are paid and calculation has also been attached. Counsel for the

State submits that the present application has been filed for
payment of retiral dues, payment of arrear of salary and grant of
ACP, admitted dues were paid but other matters need scrutiny
by the Competent Authority.
In this background, that all the admitted dues have
been paid, payment to arrear of salary and fixation of ACP is a
separate  cause  of  action.  There  is  no  pleading  relating  to
payment of arrear because even date of joining has also not been
mentioned in the present petition and at this level, it is not
possible to decide those issues.
In this view of the matter, this writ petition is disposed
off with a liberty given to the petitioner that he may move
before the Competent Authority for the claims which have not
been settled according to him till date with relevant documents.
The authority shall decide the same within 6 months from the
date of receiving the said representation.
