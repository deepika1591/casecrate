5 03-01-2023 Heard Mr. N. K. Agarwal, the learned Senior
Advocate for the petitioner and Mr. Alok Ranjan, learned
A.C. to A.A.G.-5 for the State.   
The petitioner is aggrieved by the order passed by
the licensing authority cancelling his licence on the ground
that the reply filed by him against the show-cause notice is
not satisfactory.
Mr. N. K. Agarwal grieves that no reason has been
assigned by the licensing authority for holding the reply of


Patna High Court CWJC No.23051 of 2018(5) dt.03-01-2023
2/3 
the petitioner to be unsatisfactory. In such a situation, it
would be impossible for the petitioner to even file an appeal
against the aforesaid order.
Hence the present petition. 
It has further been submitted on behalf of the
petitioner that one of the reasons which appear to have
weighed with the licensing authority is that the petitioner
was made accused in a criminal case but as on date, the
criminal proceeding against him stand quashed.
Be that as it may, considering the submission of
the petitioner that no reason has been assigned for rejecting
the reply of the petitioner, we find the order impugned to be
unsustainable in the eyes of law.
For  the  afore-noted  reason,  the  order  of
cancellation of licence dated 31.10.2012 is set aside. 
The matter is remitted to the licensing authority to
pass a fresh order in accordance with law after providing
sufficient opportunity to the petitioner to present his cause
and after adverting to the reply given by the petitioner, the
licensing authority shall pass a final order, giving reasons in
support of the same. 


Patna High Court CWJC No.23051 of 2018(5) dt.03-01-2023
3/3 
The order shall be passed within a period of 60
days, to be counted from the date of receipt/production of a
copy of this order.
The writ petition stands allowed and disposed off.
