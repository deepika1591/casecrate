Heard learned counsel for the parties. 
Petitioner has prayed for following relief (s):-
“1. That the present writ application is being
filed to direct the  respondents to return the
amount  of  security  deposit  as  well  other
amounts deducted from running on account bills
to  the  petitioner,  which  is  not  being
released/paid  by  the  respondents  for  not
completing the approach road of bridge, as said
approach road could not have been completed
since last 8 years as required land to construct


Patna High Court CWJC No.1536 of 2022 dt.04-01-2023
2/4 
the approach road of bridge is not available on
the site. Villagers are also not allowing to use
their land and till date the respondents have
failed to make available land, required for
construction of approach road and/or allow such
other relief/reliefs for which the petitioner is
found entitled in the facts and circumstances of
this case.”
For the reason that the petitioner has been pursuing
the remedies before this Court, as is also agreed by the State, the
issue of limitation shall not come in the way of consideration of
petitioner’s request on merits  if the petitioner approaches the
authorities within a period of four weeks from today, which puts
to rest the anxiety expressed by  learned counsel  for the
petitioner insofar as the merits of the present petition is
concerned.  
On 21.03.2022, we had passed the following order :-
“We are not inclined to issue notice
in the present petition and, as such, we grant
liberty to the petitioner to take recourse to such
measures  as  are  otherwise  available  in
accordance with law.
However,  we  notice  from  the
pleadings one vital issue of public interest to
have arisen, if the averments are taken to be
true. In paragraph Nos. 3 and 5, it is averred
that the period for which the works stood


Patna High Court CWJC No.1536 of 2022 dt.04-01-2023
3/4 
allotted  in  the  year  2012-13  is  yet  not
functional. Only, on this aspect, we direct the
respondent No.2, the Secretary, Rural Works
Department, Govt. of Bihar, Patna, to file his
personal affidavit.
We  clarify  that  insofar  as,  the
petitioner's case is concerned, we will not
adjudicate the same in the present petition, for
we have already granted liberty to pursue his
remedy, in accordance with law.
List on 09.05.2022.”
From the response it is evidently clear that the
Government has now, in public interest, despite the project
being  abandoned,  decided  to  complete  the  bridge  by
constructing approach roads on both the ends and make it usable
by general public.
Paragraph 19 of the affidavit filed by respondent No.
2,  namely,  Pankaj  Kumar  Pal,  Secretary,  Rural  Works
Department, Government of Bihar, to such effect is extracted
hereinunder:-
“19. That in the facts and circumstances
stated hereinabove that it is abundantly clear from
the  above  delineated  facts  that  the  present
respondent department has already taken steps for
construction of the approach road of the Bridge in
question which will be completed at the earliest.”


Patna High Court CWJC No.1536 of 2022 dt.04-01-2023
4/4 
In view of the same, we dispose of the present
petition in the following mutually agreeable terms:-
(a) The petitioner shall take recourse to the remedies
provided under the Act within a period of four weeks from
today;
(b) The same shall be decided by the appropriate
authority and issue adjudicated on merits expeditiously, within a
reasonable period of time;
(c) The bridge in question shall be made usable by
construction of approach roads on both the ends within a period
of nine months from today.
The petition stands disposed of in the aforesaid terms.
Interlocutory  Application(s),  if  any,  shall  stand
disposed of.
