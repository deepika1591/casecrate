CORAM: HONOURABLE MR. JUSTICE ANIL KUMAR SINHA
ORAL ORDER
2 03-01-2023   The petitioner has approached this court against the
order dated 13.09.2011 by which contractual services of the
petitioner as Panchayat Rojgar Sevak has been terminated vide
Memo No. 3156/D.R.D.A. dated 13.09.2011 passed by Deputy
Development Commissioner, West Champaran, Bettiah.
Learned  counsel  for  the  State  submits  that  the
petitioner has raised a whole lot of dispute in his reply to the
show-cause and there is an alternative remedy of appeal under
the scheme where District Magistrate is the Appellate Authority.


Patna High Court CWJC No.14313 of 2013(2) dt.03-01-2023
2/2 
In view of the aforesaid submission and taking into
the consideration the disputes involved in this case, this writ
application is disposed of with liberty to the petitioner to file an
appeal before the District Magistrate-cum-District Programme
Coordinator, West Champaran at Bettiah.
If the appeal is filed by the petitioner within a period
of two weeks, the District Magistrate-cum-District Programme
Coordinator, West Champaran at Bettiah shall be obliged to
dispose the same in accordance with law by a reasoned order.
It is expected that the appeal filed by the petitioner
shall be disposed by the District Magistrate, West Champaran
expeditiously, preferably within a period of three months.
