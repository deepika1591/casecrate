ORAL ORDER
2 02-01-2023 Heard learned Counsel for the parties concerned.
The  petitioner is  aggrieved by  the  order,  dated
25.01.2012, passed by the Director General of Police, Bihar,
Patna, by which 80 per cent pension of the petitioner has been
stopped, pursuant to a departmental proceeding initiated against
the  petitioner.  The  petitioner  retired  from  the  post  of
Constable/Hawaldar.
Learned Counsel for the State submits that vide memo
no. 573, dated 17.01.2017, the Director General of Police, Bihar,
Patna, has reviewed his order, dated 25.01.2012, and directed
for payment of full pension to the petitioner.


Patna High Court CWJC No.13785 of 2013(2) dt.02-01-2023
2/2 
In view of the aforesaid submission made by learned
Counsel for the State, learned Counsel for the petitioner seeks
permission to withdraw this application on the ground that the
same has, now, become infructuous.
Permission is granted.
This application is dismissed as withdrawn.
