2 03-01-2023   Petitioner has prayed for the following relief (s) :- 
 “I. For issuance of a writ in the nature of writ of
certiorari or any other appropriate writ, order or direction
for setting aside  letter dated 07.11.2019 issued by the
Territory Manager (LPG), Patna whereby the candidature
of the petitioner for award of Gramin LPG distributorship
at  Shahpur,  Gram  Panchayat-  Shahpur,  Block-
Warisaliganj,  District-  Nawada  under  OPEN  (CC)
category has been rejected and the amount of Rs. 40,000/-
deposited  by  the  petitioner  with  the  respondent
Corporation has been forfeited.
II. For issuance of writ in the nature of mandamus
or  any  other  appropriate  writ,  order  or  direction
commanding  the  respondents  to  award  Gramin  LPG
distributorship  at  Shahpur,  Gram  Panchayat  Shahpur,


Patna High Court CWJC No.25147 of 2019(2) dt.03-01-2023
2/2 
Block- Warisaliganj, District- Nawada under OPEN (CC)
category to the  petitioner for which the  petitioner has
already been declared as successful candidate in the draw
of lots conducted on 21.07.2018 for the selection of LPG
Distributor at the Shahpur, District- Nawada.
III. For the staying of further draw of lot for award
of LPG Distributor at the Shahpur, District-Nawada.
IV.   For  any  other  relief  /reliefs  for  which
petitioner is entitled in the facts and circumstances of the
case.”
After the matter was heard for some time, learned
counsel for the petitioner, under instructions, seeks permission
to withdraw the present petition reserving liberty to file a fresh
petition, on the same and subsequent cause of action with better
particulars and/or to take recourse to such other remedies as are
otherwise available in accordance with law.  
Permission granted with liberty, as prayed for.
Instant petition stands dismissed as withdrawn in
the aforesaid terms. 
Interlocutory Application(s), if any, stands disposed
of.
