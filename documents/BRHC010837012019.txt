Date : 02-01-2023
Heard Mr. N.K. Agarwal, learned Senior Advocate for
the petitioner and Mr. Upendra Pratap Singh for the State.
The license of the petitioner has been suspended
because of his having been made an accused in a criminal case
involving breach of the provisions of E.C. Act, 1955. Since the
petitioner was in jail at the time when the notice was issued to
him, the same could not be served upon him. 
The petitioner is ready and willing to reply to the
show-cause notice provided it is served upon him.


Patna High Court CWJC No.16871 of 2019 dt.02-01-2023
2/2 
The respondents are directed to serve the show-
cause notice on the petitioner within a period of fifteen days
which he shall receive positively and shall respond to it within
the next fifteen days.
The licensing authority shall after adverting to the
reply of the petitioner pass a final order within next sixty days
giving reasons in support of the decision so arrived at.
With the aforenoted direction, the petition stands
disposed of.
