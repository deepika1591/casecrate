CORAM: HONOURABLE MR. JUSTICE PRABHAT KUMAR SINGH
ORAL ORDER
3 03-01-2023 Heard the parties.
The petitioners have filed this writ petition for
following reliefs:-  


Patna High Court CWJC No.1295 of 2018(3) dt.03-01-2023
2/3 
“i.  To  direct  the  respondent  authority  to  issue  the
appointment letter in favour of the petitioners on the basis of
merit prepared by the selection unit by which the petitioners
passed the entire test of selection but lesser marks candidate
has been appointed.
ii.  To  further  direct  the  respondent  authority  to
appoint the petitioners on the post of GD Constable.
iii. For issuance of an appropriate writ (s), order (s),
direction (s) for any other relief (s) as your lordship may deem
fit and proper in the f act and circumstances of the case.”
It is submitted on behalf of the petitioners that the
petitioners appeared in the Physical Test, Written Examination
and also  in the Medical Test for recruitment to the post of
Constable in CAPFs, NIA & SSF and Rifleman (GD) in Assam
Rifles Examination, 2015 and they were declared successful but
persons having lesser marks than the petitioners have been
appointed and the case of the petitioners has not been
considered.
Counter affidavit has been filed on behalf of the
respondents- Staff Selection Commission. 
It is submitted on behalf of the respondents that no
candidate having lesser marks than the petitioners has been


Patna High Court CWJC No.1295 of 2018(3) dt.03-01-2023
3/3 
appointed. It is further submitted  that as per marks list and
write up dated 06.02.2017, the marks obtained by the petitioners
were less than the marks obtained by the last selected candidate
of his respective category and the petitioners failed to find place
in the list of recommended candidates due to their low merit in
their respective category (Annexure-R2/1 of the counter
affidavit), hence, the question of recommending the names of
the petitioners for appointment to the post of Constable (GD)
does not arise and this writ petition is liable to be dismissed at
the admission stage itself. 
Learned counsel for the petitioners has not disputed or
denied the aforesaid contention of the respondents by filing any
reply to the counter affidavit. 
Having heard the submission of the parties and on
going through the records, this court finds no merit in this writ
petition and the same is, accordingly, dismissed.
