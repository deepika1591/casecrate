(Per: HONOURABLE MR. JUSTICE ASHUTOSH KUMAR)
3 03-01-2023    Heard the learned counsel for the parties. 
  The petitioner though not an aspirant for grant of
P.D.S. license under the reserved category, has chosen to
file this petition against the grant of license to respondent
no. 8, who also does not fall in such category. 
We refuse to entertain this petition at the instance


Patna High Court CWJC No.5290 of 2020(3) dt.03-01-2023
2/2 
of the petitioner, who was not even an aspirant for the grant
of license, for which respondent no. 8 has been selected. 
It would be open for the petitioner, though, to
challenge his selection as P.D.S. licensee, in case so desired,
before the appropriate authority. 
With the aforesaid observation, the petition stands
disposed of.
